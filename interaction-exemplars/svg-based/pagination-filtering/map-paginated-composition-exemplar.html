Collection states keep recovery close

Each response preserves the controls, a clear result heading, and a route toward a valid slice. The endpoint applies one documented policy consistently.

First page

Previous becomes boundary text; valid forward destinations remain links.

Middle page

Previous, current, and next positions remain explicit and addressable.

Final page

Next becomes boundary text; a valid backward route remains available.

Zero matches

The result heading reports zero, while filters and “Clear filters” stay available.

Out of range

The endpoint returns its documented normalization or error response with a valid recovery link.
