## Units and eligible late-defect outcomes

A family groups related source-owned tables and maintained projections. A repair
batch binds one named defect or contract scope to pinned inputs, definition,
acceptance cases and a receiving review. One family can contain several batches;
one coherent dependency repair can span families. The original checkpoint remains
the first accepted family handoff or three accepted repair batches. Three families
is a separate future feasibility checkpoint, with precision and adoption assessed
through their own evidence.

An eligible handoff records the accepted revision, family/repair cluster, evidence
manifest, finding dispositions and human review decision. Before it begins, its
follow-up window receives a declared duration/end rule, severity threshold,
ascertainment plan and independent reviewer. Actual acceptance starts the window;
the record supplies the resulting start and end times. Publication is part of
acceptance when the receiving contract requires it.

Qualifying severity is blocking or material: a defect affects a required task,
accessibility, security/privacy, state integrity or another acceptance behavior
enough to require repair or changed acceptance. Cosmetic/editorial findings retain
minor standing. The independent reviewer covers the declared acceptance cases and
linked reports through the window end and adjudicates findings against the
accepted revision. Known open findings belong to handoff disposition; defects
introduced by later source changes belong to their own repair episode.

A completed eligible window contributes Y=1 for one or more qualifying late
defects discovered after its start and through its end, or Y=0 for an independently
ascertained zero. Several defects in one window still supply one indicator. An
in-progress or shortened follow-up is censored. Missing window, severity or
ascertainment data is unmeasured. Both preserve an unspecified outcome; a recorded
zero requires the completed observation. Widths, reruns, harness repairs and the
11-new/27-reused mechanism observation retain their separate units.

Current eligible outcomes are zero, so the posterior equals the illustrative
Beta(1,1) prior, mean 0.5, with standing awaiting-exposure. With f qualifying
outcomes in n completed eligible windows, Beta(a,b) becomes Beta(a+f,b+n-f).
One eligible observation updates it. A zero-defect window gives Beta(1,2); one
positive window gives Beta(2,1). These are arithmetic fixtures, not measured epoch
outcomes or new model fits. Window duration and ascertainment must be set before
any future outcome becomes eligible; the historical record supplies neither.
