# Prospective protocol: identical evidence, two execution policies

## Receiving question and freeze

For an identical accepted source, executable definition, runtime and ordered case
population, measure the local execution-cost difference between complete rerun
and exact dependency-selected reuse while all receiving evidence remains available.

This experiment answers what to rerun. Issue #320 owns when a repair batch is ready
for closure evidence. Later review/repair timing claims require prospectively
observed opportunities and a fitting eager-versus-batched design. Predictive
scheduling remains with #331's later gate.

The authored protocol precedes measurement. The freeze command binds these source
files by SHA-256 together with the concrete runtime, source manifest, ordered
population, schedule and source perturbations. The parent review is a second,
separate receipt bound to that complete freeze. A protocol, implementation,
definition, runtime or population change creates a new reviewed epoch. The current
package creates the reviewable result and launches zero measured browsers.
Revision review-02 preserves the original source/freeze in .local/bundles/review-01
and prepares its own source/freeze in .local/bundles/review-02. The original
.local/frozen.json remains intact. Revised review and completion of the parent's
application browser closure precede browser authorization; the pinned application,
population, scenario changes and launch budgets remain unchanged.

## Experimental unit, population and policies

One unit is one complete paired policy opportunity. It includes both policies
observing the same post-change application fixture under the same definition,
runtime and ordered 39-case population. The 38 prototype cases comprise
Conditioning 11, Shared Surface 15 and Investment Studio 12. The observer fixture
is the 39th case. Widths, alternate media, projection and parent cases within a
family remain correlated; neither cells nor widths become independent handoffs.

The pinned application's closure/all plan owns that ordered population. Its
existing runCampaign, executionPlan, inputSnapshot, openCheckpoints and
publishCampaign interfaces own browser observation, graph admission, checkpoint
eligibility and atomic completion. The research wrapper preserves those stages
and asserts its prospective expected selected set. It adds isolation, review
gating, copied checkpoints, material comparison and cost accounting.

Full policy runs closure/all with resume false. Selected policy runs the same
closure/all with resume true. Both start with identical copies of the same accepted
baseline generation, checkpoints and screenshots at the same fixture-relative
output path. The full policy pays its deliberate fresh execution; the selected
policy pays dependency validation, required artifact validation and generation
publication. Neither policy receives another policy's warmed checkpoint or
partially executed successor.

## Preparation, caches and seed assumption

Setup archives the pinned commit into the ignored local app fixture and copies
existing locked dependencies and Chromium installations. The primary checkout
supplies Git objects and dependency/runtime bytes; its mutable application files
never serve the experiment. The fixture contains its own source and runtime
copies. The frozen runtime includes headless-shell installation bytes as well as
the Chromium executable referenced by the production definition.

The seed is one full baseline closure. The warm-up is one further full baseline
closure from the restored seed. Both receive their own costs and precede the
900-second measured window. Their combined browser window is at most 180 seconds.
Material parity between seed and warm-up is required before timing opportunities.

Each policy uses a new Node child. Every freshly executed prototype case uses the
production harness's new browser context. Its local routes serve captured bytes;
public iframe routes receive local package bytes, and other external resources
receive the production harness's empty response. External production fixtures
and network timing are outside this experiment.

OS filesystem caches remain warm/uncontrolled. The experiment requests a quiet
machine and pins UTC and en_US.UTF-8; it performs no privileged cache flush.
Browser/profile state begins fresh for every case. Counterbalancing addresses
simple order effects without establishing independence or removing contention.
A later measurement session changes the cache condition and yields indeterminate,
while preserving all observed rows. The parent can mark a discovered contention
episode in the local state/receiving review and retain indeterminate standing.

The primary estimand conditions on an accepted seed already being available to
both policies. Seed creation is one separately reported cost. Restoring copies is
experimental preparation for both policies. Amortization over future accepted
reuse opportunities is an assumption, with no naturally observed frequency here.
A claimed net benefit would need that frequency plus development, repairs,
review and ancillary checks. These fields remain available and unestimated until
their respective observations exist.

## Material oracle and parity

The normalized material signature preserves the ordered expected inventory,
observation version, every field of every result, extracted captions/row identities,
data-label values, cells (including blank/zero/status distinctions), counts,
geometry, visibility, keyboard results, errors, assertions, verdicts and per-case
screenshot SHA-256 identities. New result fields are material by default.

Only these envelope differences leave the material signature:

- Generation version/metadata, including start timestamp, source-definition hash
  and invocation scope.
- Record storage version and signature, which identify source/definition/runtime.
- Dependency source hashes and artifact locations, which identify byte ownership.

Those identities retain separate checks. The fixture source manifest matches the
pinned tree plus the one declared suffix before and after each policy. Required
definition and family owners remain present with current hashes. The application's
publication stage validates served dependencies and newly packaged bytes. Required
screenshots remain present with correct hashes. Within a pair, dependency maps and
record signatures must also agree exactly. Full and selected material must agree
with one another and with the baseline seed.

Geometry and screenshot variability are not rounded away. A nondeterministic
difference stops timing for independent adjudication, potentially as noise rather
than an application defect. Any future normalization change receives review before
a new epoch. The seed is an accepted-source reference for identity-only perturbations;
its acceptance does not by itself validate every conceivable application assertion.

## Independently adjudicable counterexamples

faults.json is a hand-authored corpus with six literal cell states and declared
assertion results. It is separate from repaired-source timing and its expectations
come from explicit records a reviewer can inspect.

The focused Node tests load the two actual thin production owners at the pinned
revision. They exercise source, helper, runtime and missing-artifact invalidation;
omitted dependencies; stale selection state; data-label contamination; changed
assertion results; exact case membership; and interrupted publication retaining
the previous generation. Isolation fixtures restore copies after policy mutation.
Normalization fixtures require every material counterexample to remain visible.

The corpus deliberately expects the bare checkpoint reader to accept two kinds of
false evidence: an omitted source dependency, and changed result content with
otherwise matching declared inputs. A checkpoint cache checks its declared graph;
independent expected graph/material assertions reject these records. These tests
establish the distinction rather than assigning the cache an unimplemented oracle.
Fresh controls carry coherent current dependency bytes and signatures. Changed
helpers and runtimes update their declared hashes, and a missing required
screenshot is regenerated before fresh publication. Each control passes the real
inputSnapshot dependency validator, publishObservation, reopened checkpoint reader
and publishCampaign, as well as the independent material oracle. A self-comparison
alone establishes no validator acceptance. Negative fresh controls retain stale
helper/runtime hashes or a null screenshot hash and require actual rejection.

The corpus has no browser launches and does not estimate real defect prevalence
or browser detector sensitivity. A parent reviewer adjudicates each case and
consequence before authorizing the timing wave. A later DOM/browser fault-injection
wave would require a new scope, launch budget and material oracle.

## Measurement and accounting

The primary difference is full child-process wall milliseconds minus selected
child-process wall milliseconds within each pair. It includes process/import
overhead, graph preparation, execution, checkpoint/artifact operations, publication
and instrument overhead in that child. Restoring checkpoints, source isolation
checks, independent parity and review remain separate categories. Both arms use
identical instrumentation; its differential overhead remains a transfer limit.

| Field | Standing and scope |
| --- | --- |
| Browser launches | Exact successful launches from a completed production invocation |
| Document navigations | Exact production navigation counter; fixture setContent is separate |
| Case attempts/completions | Exact emitted fresh cases for a completed invocation; interrupted attempt coverage remains unmeasured |
| Ordered fresh/reused population | Exact comparison with the prospectively recorded expectation |
| uniqueReads / bytesHashed / hashMilliseconds | Exact production capture counters; fresh-validation hashing is outside these capture totals |
| freshReads | Exact named production file-read counter, including its documented validation scope |
| fsApiCalls | Exact observed calls through wrapped fs/promises methods, classified by checkpoint/artifact/other; not kernel I/O or all FileHandle writes |
| Execution/publication stage duration | Exact recorded monotonic timing scopes from the production adapter |
| Child wall duration | Parent monotonic elapsed duration including child startup/exit |
| Node peak resident KiB | resourceUsage maximum for the Node child only |
| Browser-tree peak memory | Unmeasured; Node peak stays a distinct quantity |
| Checkpoint restoration / observer work | Separate measured wrapper intervals in the local ledger |
| Development / repair / review / ancillary work | Explicit operator-supplied measurement or unmeasured standing |

All related records carry the same causal opportunity key. Failed execution
attempts retain local logs and process duration when available, with counters left
unmeasured when the production summary is incomplete. Completed first arms of
interrupted pairs stay in the cost ledger and outside the paired-difference sample.
Nested stage timings and child wall time overlap and stay separate.

## Budget, stopping and resume

The measured-phase ceiling is 900 seconds of cumulative active session time,
including checkpoint restoration and independent source/material checks. An
absolute remaining deadline bounds each child and kills its process group on
expiry. After asynchronous directory and log preparation, the runner derives
remaining time again from the absolute deadline immediately before spawn, with
no intervening await. Its kill timer uses that same absolute deadline after spawn.
An exhausted deadline admits no child and releases prepared log handles.
Cleanup and writing the stop receipt may finish after the deadline; they create
no further browser work and any elapsed overrun remains visible.

The runner stops the epoch on an unexpected selected population, failed required
assertion, missing artifact, source/runtime drift, material mismatch, incomplete
generation, interrupted child, or exhausted budget. The parent independently
classifies the stop as material defect, harness/environment limitation or noise.
The normalized disposition is indeterminate; implementation repair and another
attempt belong to a new reviewed epoch.

Terminal cancellation disposition precedes completion or adoption. SIGINT and
SIGTERM remain latched through the final child's material publication, pair
publication, final state write and lock release. Cancellation persists as an
interrupted, indeterminate outcome before the terminal summary is emitted, even
when every pair already has valid observations. Complete rows retain their
evidence; completeness never overrides the terminal disposition. The decision
contract independently treats a retained interruption as indeterminate.

Parameterized runner fixtures exercise those actual driver paths with OS signals,
delayed preparation via a controlled monotonic clock, coherent synthetic workers
and reopened decision state. They launch zero browsers and supply no measured
policy-cost observations.

Completed pairs are saved atomically. --pairs permits a deliberate pause at a pair
boundary, preserving order and remaining budget. The next invocation restores the
same seed. A process interrupted while state.active is recorded reserves the
remaining budget conservatively and stops the epoch on re-entry. Completed rows
remain available; successful rows are never silently rerun to replace a failure.

The source-derived planned totals are 30 measured invocations, 25 browser launches,
840 case attempts (820 prototype and 20 fixtures) and 1,015 document navigations.
Seed/warm-up add two launches, 78 attempts and 94 document navigations. Counts
describe a fully completed proposal; observed interruptions retain smaller or
unspecified completed coverage.

## Estimation, decision and return

For each scenario return its five ordered paired differences, arithmetic mean,
median, minimum and maximum. Also retain both policies' complete measurement rows.
A single pair is feasibility evidence. Five local sequential pairs describe this
machine/window; no confidence interval, population distribution, causal human-time
saving or general productivity estimate is claimed. Correlated widths create no
extra sample size. No outcome-based trimming or best-run selection is permitted.

The prospective conditional execution decision is:

- Defer while review or execution is pending.
- Indeterminate after a stop, incomplete 15-pair sample, multiple sessions or
  identified contention.
- Adopt the selected policy for this local condition if every paired difference
  is positive in all three scenarios and material requirements pass.
- Retain full execution if every paired difference is nonpositive across all
  scenarios and material requirements pass.
- Combine when a complete valid result has mixed signs; report the per-scenario
  distribution and leave broader adoption with the receiver.

These rules concern the observed conditional cost only; the full range and
uncertainty remain visible even for adopt/retain. Shared-helper selection may cost
slightly more because every case needs fresh evidence; that outcome is admissible.
