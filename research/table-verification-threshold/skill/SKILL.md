# Protective verification window

## What

A revisable distinction between discovery, repair, verification and handoff that keeps a passing check attached to its actual scope and revision.

## When

Repeated broad checks, shifting source owners, late coverage discoveries or premature closure make the next observation difficult to choose.

## Boundaries

Mandatory checks and human authority remain in force. Probability models describe assumptions and observed transitions; an unresolved gate remains unresolved at any predicted probability. Necessary discovery and proportionate focused reruns are valid work.

## Result

One closure candidate or one named uncertainty, a fitting check scope, an explicit reopening route and an honest handoff boundary.

## Rules

1. Name the agreed behavior, source/projection owners, relevant states and evidence required before treating a check as a closure gate.
2. Separate a discovery question from an acceptance assertion. Choose the smallest observation that can change the next decision.
3. Consolidate a coherent affected set and review it before the broad gate. Record changes arriving after that candidate rather than silently extending its scope.
4. A late finding names its owning repair and the evidence it invalidates. Retain still-applicable observations and rerun affected dependencies.
5. Complete mandatory final evidence after the last relevant behavior change, then hand back the bounded scope and revision. Later findings become explicit follow-up episodes.
6. Preserve completed cases through harness failures. Count harness attempts, focused campaigns, full campaigns and late application findings separately.
7. Use probabilities as sensitivity-aware decision support with named units, priors, missing data and dependence assumptions. Models leave observed readiness criteria intact.
8. Evaluate protection through coverage, time to discovery, premature closure and late findings. Invocation counts are secondary; fewer checks can reflect weaker observation.
9. Keep actual results distinct from simulated scenarios and modeled baseline savings. An indeterminate result is a useful research return.

## Completion

The receiving collaborator can identify the current process state, remaining uncertainty, next meaningful observation and event that would reopen evidence. This candidate earns portable/public promotion through reviewed natural-use observations rather than a favorable demonstration alone.
