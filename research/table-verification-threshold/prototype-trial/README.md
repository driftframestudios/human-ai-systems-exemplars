# Prototype-assisted decision trial: opportunity 1 of at most 3

## Before use

The selected route was already a protective verification window: map owners/states,
run decision-changing focused checks, form and review a closure candidate, complete
its affected-family gate, and reopen only dependent evidence after later findings.
The trial asked whether existing instruments would change that route or reveal a
missing decision distinction.

## Actual use

The canonical Strategy Canvas v0.2.5 accepted a schema-valid working state with
three anchored dimensions: coverage boundary, evidence reopening and decision yield.
Its native Copy JSON control emitted the saved `strategy-canvas.json`. The canonical
PODA v0.1.5 accepted and re-emitted `poda.json`, linking purpose to two objectives,
two discoverables and two activities. An isolated copy receiver captured output;
this was state-transfer observation rather than an operating-system clipboard test.

The Strategy Canvas schema made ordinal scores, consequence weights and review
standing explicit. The trial retained `working` status and AI-authored judgments.
PODA supplied navigable objective/discoverable/activity links. These are observed
representation benefits, not proof of better decisions.

## After use and disposition

**Selected policy changed: no.** The instruments organized the existing decision;
no additional consequential defect or architectural choice was established by the
trial. Routine repeated use on this same question is released. The record remains
useful for a later receiving collaborator, with that reuse still unobserved.

A first connection attempt met the local server's sandbox restriction. A later
trial attempted to fill an intentionally closed import disclosure; opening the
native disclosure completed transfer. These are trial-environment/harness costs,
not evidence that either prototype's product behavior is defective.

One bounded epoch remains available: at most two additional distinct opportunities,
for a total of three. Candidate 2 is Specification-by-Example on the first concrete
table regression; candidate 3 is First Responsible Version Matrix on a family
handoff boundary. Begin only when the instrument can resolve a named uncertainty.
Record the direct-reasoning baseline, post-use change, cost and receiving reuse.
Continue, revise or abandon based on decision-changing value rather than a target
number of completed artifacts. A three-opportunity result supports local fit
assessment, not a general efficacy claim.
