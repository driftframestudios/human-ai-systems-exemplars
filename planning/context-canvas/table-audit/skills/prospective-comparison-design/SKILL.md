# Prospective comparison design

## Contract

- **What:** A bounded comparison protocol whose measured outcomes can change a named receiving decision.
- **When:** Alternative execution policies or collaboration methods invite a claim about selection, cost, protection, prediction or transfer.
- **Boundaries:** Descriptive records, prospective predictions, interventions and simulations retain distinct evidence classes. Required acceptance gates keep their authority. Protocol design, execution, model updating and operational adoption have separate receiving decisions.
- **Result:** A reviewable target quantity, unit, baseline, eligible exposure, observation window, budget and criterion for adoption, revision or rest.

## Standing

Draft contract with bounded receiving exercises. Current observations support
their stated local interpretation; wider calibration and adoption remain
separately gated.

## Fit and attention

- **Useful when:** A plausible count difference or model output could otherwise be mistaken for evidence about safety, saved work or future performance.
- **Poor fit when:** A descriptive inventory is sufficient, or a known acceptance defect already has a deterministic repair and required check.
- **Smallest useful dose:** One decision, one outcome and denominator, one declared comparator, one eligibility rule and one bounded receiving return.
- **Foregrounds:** Prospective timing, comparable conditions, distinct outcome oracles, censoring and the separation of updating from adoption.
- **May under-attend:** A well-specified comparison can still miss an important visitor consequence or carry weak ascertainment; those uncertainties retain named receivers.
- **Material result:** An executable bounded protocol or a decision to retain description until the missing observation becomes available.
- **Interactions:** Checkpointed campaigns supply execution records; source authority identifies versions; cost analysis supplies measurement boundaries; human review owns consequential acceptance and adoption.
- **Rest or release when:** The comparison can serve its receiving decision, or a missing outcome, exposure or authority has a concrete trigger. Additional modeling follows a changed signal.

## Rules

### Rule: The receiving decision defines the target quantity

A comparison states the decision it could change and the event, population and
horizon of any probability. Delivery estimates remain subjective planning priors
until comparable outcomes support calibration. A descriptive result or an
indeterminate comparison can complete the research return.

### Rule: Outcomes keep their units and oracles

Selection correctness, defect detection, late-defect risk, execution cost and
human workload receive separate observations. Launches, attempts, completed
cases, page visits, journeys, repair batches and accepted handoffs keep their
denominators. Repeated widths and retries retain their shared repair identity;
their count alone supplies neither independent exposure nor human effort.

### Rule: Prospective declarations precede their outcomes

The policy, starting state, versions, expected case set, measurement rules and
stop criteria are recorded before execution. Later interpretation carries its
own annotation time and retrospective standing. Original events retain their
meaning and order; a revised schema separates historical entry from current
summary, and a changed observation definition identifies its new coverage.

### Rule: Eligible exposure has an ascertainment contract

An eligible outcome names its acceptance event, source revision, scope, window,
severity rule and independent ascertainment. A completed-window indicator uses
completed eligible windows; in-progress or shortened follow-up remains censored,
and missing required observation remains unmeasured. A recorded zero requires
the complete observation. Pre-handoff findings and later introduced defects
retain their own episodes rather than entering a different risk denominator.

### Rule: Comparable replay and defect detection have different jobs

Paired policies share equivalent inputs, observation definitions, populations
and required evidence. Setup, cache conditions, execution order, repetitions
and maximum resource budget are declared before comparison. Repaired-source
replay measures operational cost under those conditions; independently
adjudicated fault cases test detection. Each claim receives the matching design.

### Rule: Updating uses the declared outcomes

A probability model receives only outcomes admitted by its unit and exposure
rule. One eligible Bernoulli observation can update a prior; zero eligible
observations leave that prior unchanged. A complete observed zero differs from
an absent outcome. Posterior updating, precision, calibration, transport to a
new population and operational adoption remain distinct decisions.

### Rule: Prediction remains a separate authority boundary

A prospective shadow prediction retains all required checks and records its
forecast before outcomes become available. A proposal to change operational
checking requires its own authorized adoption decision, reviewed severity-aware
risk and precision criteria, suitable held-out evidence and fallback. The
comparison itself supplies evidence rather than execution or publication
permission.

### Rule: Claims return at the strength of their evidence

An observed policy difference supports the named comparison at its measured
scope. Net savings include the setup, maintenance, recovery and review terms
needed by that decision. Unsupported effects remain unestimated with a useful
measurement route. One receiving surface carries the normalized result, its
scope and the next decision; a new model or recurring study earns a separate
receiving use.

## Boundary scenarios

### Scenario: Reuse changes case execution but supplies no eligible handoff

- **Given:** A resume runs a selected subset and reuses matching observations.
- **When:** The comparison summarizes that invocation before any eligible handoff window completes.
- **Then:** Selection and execution counts remain descriptive outcomes, the late-defect prior remains unchanged, and a cost claim routes to matched measurement.

### Scenario: A follow-up window closes early

- **Given:** A declared completed-window indicator requires independent ascertainment through its end.
- **When:** Follow-up stops early without a complete eligible observation.
- **Then:** The window retains censored standing and an unspecified indicator; continued ascertainment or a separately specified model owns the next move.

These scenarios explain eligibility branches. Arithmetic fixtures and observed
research outcomes retain their distinct evidence roles.

## Completion and re-entry

The target decision, comparator, units, prospective boundary, eligible exposure,
budget and receiving criterion are explicit, or the result names the missing
observation that makes further modeling premature. Attention returns to the
material decision. New eligible outcomes, changed definitions, an independent
counterexample or a newly authorized adoption question can reopen the fitting
comparison without turning every execution into a research campaign.
