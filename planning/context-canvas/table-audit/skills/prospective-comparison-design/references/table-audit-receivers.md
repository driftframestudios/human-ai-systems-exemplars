## Local interpretation

The epoch separates its immutable entry, original event ledger, retrospective
annotations, versioned observation definitions and current summary. A planned
definition acquires execution evidence through a fresh event at its actual
source/runtime identity. Reused cases retain the definition under which they
were observed.

The existing late-defect model is an illustrative Beta-Bernoulli contract.
For f qualifying outcomes among n completed eligible windows, Beta(a,b) becomes
Beta(a+f,b+n-f). With n=0, the posterior equals the prior. A completed observed
zero contributes one eligible outcome; censored and unmeasured indicators keep
their unspecified standing. Several qualifying defects in one window contribute
one indicator. Synthetic arithmetic fixtures establish those example rules;
actual accepted handoffs and follow-up records establish empirical exposure.

Selection agreement, unchanged resume and passing material checks supply their
named application evidence. Comparison of execution costs, measured workload,
late-defect exposure and calibrated prediction each needs its own observation.
Existing records own the current launch, attempt, page-visit and journey totals;
this reference adds no second denominator or mutable scorecard.

## Authority and release

The original declared comparison remains a historical reference. A future replay
or prediction trial records its protocol and stopping event before execution.
Observed improvement, model updating, accepted risk and adoption remain separate
decisions. This local binding releases when the research receiver can interpret
the return or names the missing exposure; a new eligible observation or approved
comparison can reopen it.
