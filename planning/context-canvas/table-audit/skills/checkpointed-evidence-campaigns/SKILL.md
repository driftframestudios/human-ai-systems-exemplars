# Checkpointed evidence campaigns

## Contract

- **What:** A bounded observation campaign whose completed cases retain useful evidence across interruption, repair and resumption.
- **When:** Independently useful cases share an observer or environment, and retaining matching observations changes the next execution decision.
- **Boundaries:** Source owners define acceptance and current user authority governs execution and publication. A checkpoint establishes a named observation of named inputs. Dependency completeness, campaign completion, acceptance and predictive selection each retain their own evidence and authority.
- **Result:** A receiver can identify requested, newly observed, reused, failed and pending cases, the identity supporting each observation, and the smallest remaining execution or repair.

## Standing

Draft contract with bounded receiving exercises. Those observations inform
revision; catalog promotion and broader adoption retain separate review.

## Fit and attention

- **Useful when:** Cases remain independently valuable and repeated execution, interruption or changed dependencies creates a material recovery cost.
- **Poor fit when:** A cheap indivisible check already answers the question, or the receiving job is deployment, statistical calibration or a general artifact store.
- **Smallest useful dose:** One frozen requested inventory, source-bound case records and an explicit resume decision, using existing storage and observation owners.
- **Foregrounds:** Input identity, complete required artifacts, dependency-sensitive invalidation and the difference between case recovery and campaign publication.
- **May under-attend:** A precise identity can still omit a dependency; a completed campaign can still miss a visitor task outside its declared coverage.
- **Material result:** A bounded coverage return with retained evidence and a named owner for each consequential gap.
- **Interactions:** Source-authority mapping locates dependencies; the behavior or visual contract defines observations; cost analysis accounts for work; prospective comparison design owns claims about policy effects.
- **Rest or release when:** The authorized return is usable, or a pending case has a concrete receiver and changed-signal trigger. Additional infrastructure follows a demonstrated receiving need.

## Rules

### Rule: Requested coverage precedes execution

A campaign freezes its ordered requested cases, intentional exclusions, required
verdicts and artifact requirements before acquiring execution resources. Stable
case identity includes the receiving behavior and expected content population.
Candidate enumeration, selected execution and retained coverage have separate
counts. A family or exact-case request retains that declared boundary.

### Rule: Identity and dependency completeness earn different observations

Reuse compares source and projection inputs, observer and executable helpers,
relevant data, parameters, environment and required artifacts. A matching digest
establishes equality within the declared closure. Source tracing and a fitting
dependency perturbation establish whether that closure covers the claim. An
uncertain shared owner broadens the observation or returns to discovery.

### Rule: Capture has one admitted input generation

Each in-flight capture has a stable identity and an explicit completion state.
Concurrent consumers can share the same pending capture when their identities
agree; a retained rejection has an explicit retry owner. Publication binds to an
immutable captured generation or a final input agreement check. Source drift
returns the affected case for a fresh identity while prior observations retain
their historical meaning.

### Rule: Case completion includes its artifacts

Required assertions and artifacts complete before a case checkpoint becomes
reusable. Temporary writes remain distinguishable from accepted records; a
supported atomic publication operation commits the complete case. Interruption
preserves earlier accepted evidence, and a retry owns a fresh temporary write.
Attempt summaries describe invocations separately from cumulative case evidence.

### Rule: Campaign completion derives from the requested inventory

A successful produced subset supplies evidence for those cases. Whole-campaign
completion additionally reconciles exact requested membership, required verdicts
and validated artifacts. Per-case completion and publication of a complete
generation are separate events. Failures retain observations and distinct
application, observer or environment repair owners; the acceptance decision
remains with the receiving contract.

### Rule: Invalidation reasons accumulate

Selection evaluates missing evidence, changed source or projections, helper or
dependency changes, changed definitions, environment or parameters, required
artifacts and uncertain boundaries. All applicable reasons remain visible, and
their case IDs form a union in requested order. Unrelated complete observations
remain available. A changed definition gives new evidence its own scope while
earlier records remain historical observations.

### Rule: Recovery is exercised at its consequential boundary

The smallest relevant positive and negative observer fixtures precede reliance
on a changed assertion. A fitting interruption/resume exercise shows retention
of complete cases, renewal of incomplete or changed cases and an honest campaign
state. Evidence for exact content, state transitions and artifact publication is
chosen by the behavior being protected; a broad repeat needs a changed signal.

### Rule: Counts support their own receiving claims

Campaigns, launches, attempts, completions, reused cases, retries, navigations and
material journeys retain their units. A reuse demonstration establishes recovery
mechanics. Cost savings and predictive performance require their own comparison
and outcome design. The durable return contains normalized results, scope and
the next decision; raw execution material stays with its existing local or
access-controlled owner.

## Boundary scenarios

### Scenario: Every produced case passes but one requested case is absent

- **Given:** A campaign requests four cases and publishes three complete observations.
- **When:** All three produced verdicts pass.
- **Then:** Those cases retain recovery value, campaign completion remains pending, and the missing case has a direct execution receiver.

### Scenario: An omitted dependency acquires an owner

- **Given:** A stored identity matches its listed inputs but a loaded dependency was omitted.
- **When:** Source tracing or a perturbation exposes the missing dependency.
- **Then:** Corrected identities reopen dependent observations, preserve unrelated evidence and retain earlier records with their original scope.

These scenarios clarify intended behavior; the conditional exercise owner records
which boundaries have actually been observed.

## Completion and re-entry

The skill completes when every requested case has a legible state, supported
reuse is distinguishable from new execution, campaign completion has its own
disposition, and one receiving surface carries remaining work. A pending campaign
can have a complete handback. Changed inputs, a missing dependency, an incomplete
artifact or a newly relevant acceptance behavior reopens only the fitting scope.
Attention returns to the material task; broader scheduling or storage adoption
requires its own receiving decision.
