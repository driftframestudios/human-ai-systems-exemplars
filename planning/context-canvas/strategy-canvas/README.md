## Two expressions, one semantic spine

- **Canonical worksheet:** a quiet, reference-derived HTML, CSS, SVG, and JavaScript artifact. It documents instruction implications, scale meaning, state identity, import/export behavior, and accessibility equivalents. Its styling remains intentionally straightforward to support downstream extension.
- **Context Canvas workbench:** a facilitation instrument for turn-taking, shared readings, reversible proposals, notes, evidence, review, and handoff. Its interface speaks to the present collaboration. Architecture lineage and production history remain in this planning surface and in threadkeeping.

Both expressions use `strategy-canvas/2.0` semantics. A current reading, good-enough threshold, desired reading, participant ballot, derived group reading, reversible scenario, and reviewed decision retain distinct identities.

## Authority and evidence boundary

The canvas makes a comparative hypothesis inspectable. Participants define the factors, anchors, evidence standing, and decision horizon. A calculated aggregate summarizes submitted readings; it carries the authority assigned by the facilitation agreement. AI contributions arrive as attributed proposals or bounded research material. A reviewed decision records the human or institution able to accept it.

## Shared completion gate

The family is ready for review when:

- the canonical and Context expressions validate against the same semantic contract;
- every score control has a dimension-specific written label and declared scale anchors;
- participant ballots, aggregates, scenarios, and reviewed values remain separately recoverable;
- a pointer drag has tap, button, and native-input equivalents;
- a participant completes a sealed turn, then the group readings appear;
- the group view communicates count, missingness, center, and spread;
- every compatibility view agrees: the no-script table, SVG, JSON export, print view, and reviewed report;
- imports identify their source dialect and migration result;
- all user-authored text is rendered as text;
- 390, 1440, 1920, and 2560 CSS-pixel treatments preserve a usable working surface;
- keyboard, pointer, forced-colors, reduced-motion, reciprocal zoom-reflow, and print checks pass; touch hardware and actual browser/assistive-technology zoom retain explicit manual receipts;
- Context Canvas copy describes the active collaboration and its available actions;
- canonical copy documents instructions and their material implications.
