Good next question: Which dimensions define stronger support for this decision, and what condition is adequate for the next responsible phase?

Instruction implications for human and AI collaborators
  • Define scale anchors before requesting scores. An observable low, middle, and high condition gives the number a shared local meaning.
  • Keep offering level and consequence weight distinct. Offering level moves the curve. Consequence weight directs discussion attention while leaving the plotted score unchanged.
  • Carry participant readings and spread with the summary. A mean becomes more useful when the number of readings, distribution, abstentions, and materially different views remain inspectable.
  • Ask when task position or evidence standing remains open. Clarify which decision this canvas supports, which horizon it serves, and what evidence gives a current-state reading its standing.
  • Treat good enough as the next-phase threshold. Desired describes the stronger future condition; good enough marks the condition adequate for the named horizon.
  • Apply the action lens after the baseline shape is visible. Eliminate, Reduce, Raise, and Create become interpretations of an inspectable configuration.
  • Keep AI suggestions visibly attributed. AI-generated factors, anchors, scores, and interpretations enter as proposals outside human aggregates until an explicit review accepts them.
  • Choose the AI handoff scope explicitly. Assemble a packet from the reviewed fields participants have placed in scope. This local prototype performs the assembly in the browser and makes no network request.
