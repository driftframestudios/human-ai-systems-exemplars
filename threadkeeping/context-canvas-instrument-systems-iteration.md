## Visitor-facing invariants

- Page prose explains the work a visitor can do, the people affected, the changed ability, and the remaining boundary.
- Collection counts appear only when they materially orient a choice.
- Animation names different decision shapes; the visitor's situation determines selection.
- Motion keeps the full evidence state available, freezes under reduced motion, and has a static print posture.
- State travels through text and geometry as well as color.
- Source-native vocabulary remains attached to the page or instrument that earned it.
- An observed anomaly means `LOOK HERE` only when the affected surface and responsible receiver remain adjacent.
- Human authority, evidence scope, and unresolved work remain explicit without adding rankings, readiness scores, or collaborator-confidence values.
- Ordinary links own navigation. Embedded prototypes provide semantic and no-script equivalents.
