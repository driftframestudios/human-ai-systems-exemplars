## Working observation

The Context Canvas language review exposed a job narrower than ordinary-language editing and broader than replacing one label. Names, role metaphors, agency verbs, technical functions, interface copy, serialized presentation strings, source-owned quotations, and human authority needed to remain distinguishable across authored pages and generated iframe carriers.

The Practitioner Registry's exercised skill family supplied a useful separation: cue-bundle diagnosis, identity and role calibration, language translation, and causal evaluation are different receiving jobs. This repository use composes those distinctions with existing source-authority mapping, Context Canvas surface reading, and supportive change writing.

The concrete exercised examples were:

- **Audit Anthropomorphic Cues — Inspect the Cue Bundle Before Changing the Interface:** diagnose the combined cues and their reliance implications before editing.
- **Calibrate AI Names and Role Language:** distinguish product or system identity, interaction alias, local function, and accountable authority.
- **Translate Anthropomorphic Language Precisely:** carry the distinction into visitor-readable wording without flattening warmth or source fidelity.
- **Evaluate Persona and Reliance Effects:** reserve causal claims for a separately designed comparison when a consequential decision actually depends on the effect.

Only the first job lacked a repository owner. The other three remain with existing language, source-authority, and future evaluation routes rather than becoming a new skill set by accumulation.

## Skill disposition

`audit-anthropomorphic-cues` receives `create_now`. It owns the distinct result: a cue-bundle ledger and proportionate retain, qualify, separate, disclose, test, or revise decisions. `supportive-change-writing` retains final prose ownership. `source-authority-mapping` retains source and projection lineage. A causal persona or reliance comparison remains a future evaluation candidate because this language migration does not supply experimental evidence.

The first version stays instruction-only. The current page-family and iframe migration supplies natural-use evidence without creating a new lint dependency, authority model, or generated ledger.

## Evidence boundary

This exercise establishes a useful repository route for distinguishing person-like cues, technical functions, compatibility language, and human authority across one bounded public-surface family. It does not establish a universal effect for a name, metaphor, persona, or role label. The skill remains public-possible and repository-only until a separate publication decision receives its self-contained source package.
