## Working moment

A visitor-surface contrast repair expanded into a principal-level convergence cycle before commit. The human valued the existing Context Canvas visual language and asked for a durable answer to light text on light paint, blue links on cobalt, rule necessity, cascade architecture, modern CSS, viewports, reading fatigue, scanning behavior, and AI interpretation. Prototype and iframe-document interiors retained their existing source owners.

Spark usage was exhausted, so no Spark support request was attempted. Three full-capability reviewers independently examined cascade architecture, interoperability and adversarial states, and visual/cognitive behavior. A second round required counterexamples and final retain/refactor/defer dispositions.

## Material implementation observation

The two-level custom-property architecture held through that cycle:

- four immutable light/dark unvisited/visited palette roles;
- two inherited active roles;
- complete active pairs at painted owners;
- complete resets at nested background changes; and
- route-specific active values where art direction earns them.

This architecture repaired the principal cobalt-link concern while retaining the route family. It also made four adjacent conditions visible: print role leakage, a low-contrast salmon hover, sub-24px compact rail targets, and a table caption clipped to its wide table inside a narrow scrollport.

The human approved the architecture and explicitly treated the net production increase as high leverage. That correction matters to skill cultivation: line count remains an implementation observation, while ownership clarity, observable behavior, regression sensitivity, and future extension provide the decision criteria.
