## Working moment

The collaboration began with a broad question: how to add unit tests across two
heterogeneous prototype families while respecting artifact ownership, branch
topology, Actions budgets, and the Practitioner Registry's testing posture.

A source audit found the consequential route: two genuine DOM-independent
modules, one live Composer/schema mismatch, three strict-schema gaps, one absent
Narrative pack reference, and Core manifest identity drift. The first draft had
already produced a rich assessment schema, a second research payload, three
milestones, and eleven issues ahead of the first runnable unit test.

That evidence changed the work. The schema/example and secondary payload were
removed. Four issues that shared existing owners or awaited evidence are routed
into those receivers. The retained plan begins with direct `node:test`, Ajv,
and existing Playwright behavior; the existing architecture remains the
receiving layer.

The PR review then exposed a reusable writing gap. Several evidence boundaries
led with the adjacent claim's limit while leaving their affirmative scope
implicit. The named Semantic Attractor guidance supplied the stronger form:
state what the signal establishes, treat constitutive silence as an unspecified
state, and route the adjacent claim to fitting evidence.

## Formation return — stateful artifact behavior contracts

`collaboration-accounting` selects `create_now`. Four materially different
receivers established one job that the standing catalog left unowned:
selecting the smallest source-grounded behavior contract for a stateful
artifact exchange while the artifact retains semantic authority.

The resulting `stateful-artifact-behavior-contracts` skill foregrounds
self-roundtrip validity, minimum usable state, semantic-twin agreement,
detached exchange state, stable identity or chronology, and retained-state
recovery. Direct tests carry isolated logic and schema boundaries; existing
browser harnesses carry rendered interaction and exchange. Hosted execution
continues through the configurable receiver and returns to rest after a named
receipt.

Issue work:cross-prototype-exchange is the first outside receiver. Its independently owned producer,
adapter, and consumer boundaries can test whether the contract stays useful
across artifacts without flattening ownership. That material result can revise,
rest, fold, or retain the skill through the next collaboration-accounting
return.

An independent read-only forward test supplied the first narrow revision. The
skill selected the canonical Strategy Canvas v2 export → Narrative Composer
adapter exchange and predicted a shallow claimed-dialect check plus lost source
lineage. Its route also clarified four distinctions now carried by the portable
contract: producer and receiver schemas retain separate assertions; source
identity remains lineage while receiver identity stays receiver-owned; a
dedicated orchestration journey carries boundary-only assertions across family
owners; and read-only fail-first work stops with observable evidence and a
named repair owner.

## First outside use — Core to Narrative exchange

Issue work:cross-prototype-exchange exercised the formed contract across independently governed
artifacts. A real labelled score edit traveled from the canonical Core Strategy
Canvas serializer through the Narrative adapter into the Composer's rendered
state, governed composition, and receiver-owned Strategy v2 projection. One
claimed-v2 candidate with a missing source identity tested the rejection route.

The practice changed implementation choices. Core schema validity stayed a
producer assertion, Narrative composition validity stayed a receiver
assertion, and the adapter received only recognition, ordering, translation,
and lineage responsibilities. The dedicated orchestration journey therefore
contains boundary assertions rather than duplicating either family suite. An
older hostile fixture moved back to the outbound-normalization unit where its
meaning belongs; the rendered safety case now uses a schema-valid source with a
markup-like literal label.

Material use exposed one addition to the portable contract. Core working-state
edits can share artifact identity and revision, so those coordinates alone do
not identify the exchanged state. The lineage receipt now adds a named payload
fingerprint, and the skill states that a source with revision-shared working
states needs either such a fingerprint or the exact source snapshot. The
focused skill route remains sufficient for the exchange.
