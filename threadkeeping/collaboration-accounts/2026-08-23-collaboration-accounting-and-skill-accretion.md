The next observation arrived from that material return. A standing skill could retain its durable contract while the live CSS tranche received a narrower, temporary form. The existing `collaboration-accounting` skill owned the lifecycle question, so the primary route was `iterate_now`.

A targeted Notion pass clarified the terms. A **portable skill contract** is the operative source. A **task-bound skill projection** binds selected source behavior to one receiving task and lifetime. A **non-routable context capsule** carries deeper local detail without adding another automatic routing entry. A **binding profile** keeps receiving task, source contract, selected behavior, platform and tools, authority, lifetime, disposition, and upstream return separate.

The recursion guard is `max_routable_projection_depth: 1`. The scalar describes lineage only. A depth-one projection produces capsules, accounts, completion evidence, or upstream proposals. A distinct transferable job returns through `create_now` as a new depth-zero portable contract.
