## Name and ownership decision

`Notion two-key receiver` is a useful situated description. Its terms bind the move to one vendor, imply two equivalent keys, and name one receiver where the material topology contains two. `Runner` suggests deterministic automation that the current judgment-led work has yet to earn. `Folding` names one transformation within the job.

`paired-receiver settlement` is the current operator-blind working name. **Paired receiver** identifies the topology. **Settlement** names the observable close and is defined here as an evidence-bearing collaboration receipt. The phrase carries collaboration meaning rather than a financial settlement or an atomic distributed transaction.

## Transfer boundary and forge condition

The observed uses all cross a locator or publication boundary, so they establish a narrow revision to the existing owner. A later use can earn `paired-receiver-settlement` as an independent portable contract when paired acceptance is itself the durable job outside this terrain—for example, complementary schema and package receivers or two public receivers with different accepted projections—and the current skill composition leaves part of that result unowned.

The route reopens when that outside use appears, a receiver-specific acceptance state proves insufficient, or a human correction changes the meaning of settlement. Until then, the named method remains an enrichment inside locator-boundary stewardship and attention returns to the source-settlement work.
