# Agency and action transformations

These examples demonstrate editorial reasoning rather than mandatory sentence templates. Each revision uses only information present in its example.

## Restore the author’s contribution

**Before**

> The participant-authored account reports that the profiles removed recurring assignment errors.

**After**

> My work on the profiles eliminated recurring assignment errors.

**Why:** The original sentence refers to its own author from a distance and makes the account, rather than the work, carry the claim. The revision restores the actor, action, object, and consequence. “Eliminated” belongs only where the evidence supports that strength; otherwise use “reduced.”

## Replace an entry metaphor with the first assignment

**Before**

> I entered through a search redesign before the program expanded.

**After**

> My first assignment was redesigning search; the program later expanded.

**Why:** “Entered through” describes a spatial relationship. “My first assignment” tells an evaluative reader what happened and preserves sequence.

Other accurate variants include:

- “I joined the program to redesign search.”
- “I began by redesigning search.”
- “The search redesign was my first assignment.”

The surrounding paragraph determines which rhythm fits.

## Name the work behind a nominalization

**Before**

> An assessment of the workflow was undertaken, leading to the identification of duplicate approvals.

**After**

> The operations team reviewed the workflow and found duplicate approvals.

**Why:** The revision turns two nominalizations into actions and identifies the established actor. If the source does not identify the team, preserve that uncertainty: “A workflow review found duplicate approvals.”

## Keep collective agency accurate

**Before**

> I built and launched the clinical coordination system.

**After**

> I led the system design; engineering built the application, and clinical operations prepared the launch.

**Why:** Direct voice also clarifies contribution boundaries. The revision distinguishes design leadership, implementation, and operational adoption rather than converting a team result into individual ownership.

## Preserve the affected object in first position

**Before**

> The support team notified every customer whose record the migration damaged.

**After**

> Every affected customer received a direct notice from the support team.

**Why:** Active voice is not a universal target. The second construction keeps affected customers first, names the responsible team, and states the completed support action.

## Replace a generic surface with the actual interface

**Before**

> I improved the clinical-trial coordination surface.

**After**

> I redesigned the trial-setup interface so data managers could reuse validated rules.

**Why:** In the original, *surface* hides both the artifact and the work. The revision names the interface, action, user, and consequence.

## Retain surface when it names a real boundary

**Before**

> The genomic viewer became a specialist decision-making surface.

**After**

> The genomic viewer became a specialist decision-making surface where pathologists could compare evidence and record an interpretation.

**Why:** *Surface* contributes meaning here: it joins information, judgment, and action. The revision retains the abstraction and makes its function concrete.

## Use the noun represented by a table column

**Before**

> Participant surface

Rows describe what participants see, understand, and do.

**After**

> Participant experience

**Why:** The row values define an experience rather than a delivery plane or decision boundary. The label now names the relationship represented by the column.

## State the relevant judgment without a candor performance

**Before**

> To be completely honest, I was frustrated by the handoff and felt that I had to intervene.

**After**

> The handoff left the decision unresolved, so I brought the two teams together to settle ownership.

**Why:** The revision preserves the consequential judgment and action. It leaves personal feeling available when that feeling matters to the story, while avoiding a ritual claim of candor.

## Keep uncertainty when the actor is unknown

**Before**

> The migration team removed three fields from the archive.

**After when the actor is unverified**

> Three fields were missing from the archive after migration.

**Why:** Restoring agency never licenses invention. The passive construction accurately foregrounds the observed state and temporal boundary. A later source can establish who changed the archive.

## Fast editorial diagnostic

For each candidate passage, ask:

| Element | Question | Useful repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actor | Can the reader identify who or what acted? | Name the person, team, role, system, or process supported by the source. |
| Action | Does the main verb describe the work? | Replace entry metaphors and nominalizations with the actual verb. |
| Object | Can the reader recognize what changed? | Name the interface, workflow, decision, artifact, or condition. |
| Consequence | Does the sentence explain why the action mattered? | Add the established effect, capability, or decision. |
| Boundary | Does the claim preserve shared agency and evidence strength? | Distinguish contributions, retain uncertainty, and calibrate the verb. |
| Voice | Does directness fit the artifact? | Choose first person, collective, third person, or impersonal form deliberately. |

A passage is ready when the diagnostic can be answered from the prose itself or when the prose makes a deliberate uncertainty or abstraction legible.
