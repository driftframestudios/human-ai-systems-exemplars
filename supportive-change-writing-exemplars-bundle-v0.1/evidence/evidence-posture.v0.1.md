# Evidence-aware writing

Research deepens interpretation while remaining proportionate to its warrant. Primary studies and high-quality reviews supply concepts, associations, mechanisms, and bounded causal evidence. Author-year citations place that context inside readerly prose.

## Calibration

- Match claim strength to study design, sample, setting, and inferential reach.
- Distinguish individual, team, organizational, and institutional levels.
- Keep related constructs conceptually distinct.
- Translate each concept into a practical, relational, or structural consequence.
- Give workload, resources, authority, status, access, and unequal exposure analytic weight.

## How sources appear

Each branch carries a reference file with the sources cited in its essays. The [branch reference map](branch-reference-sets.v0.1.yaml) connects those files with the main evidence domains used in each branch.

Evidence remains a companion to lived experience and practical judgment. The essays state uncertainty where it matters and connect research concepts with recognizable workplace situations.
