# Latent-quality embodiment assessment

## Probability referent

Each estimate is the editorially judged likelihood that a blind reviewer recognizes all five latent qualities in the essay: reliable support and belonging; resourceful adaptation using available strengths; clear reciprocal communication; care translated into practical inclusion; and realistic hope expressed through flexibility and incremental progress.

The values express comparative editorial judgment. They carry a shared referent and appear in the corresponding essay metadata.

## Editorial selection probabilities

The branch architecture uses complementary postures. Their selection probabilities estimate which posture is most likely to provide useful comparative enrichment for the author:

- Integrative practical synthesis — `0.50`
- Critical-systems counterpoint — `0.30`
- Human-scale developmental or horizon lens — `0.20`

## Reading the range

Higher values indicate a more explicit presence of all five qualities. Lower values often arise where an essay gives greater space to power, structural critique, institutional responsibility, or evidence boundaries. The range supports comparative literacy by showing several credible ways to carry the underlying qualities.
