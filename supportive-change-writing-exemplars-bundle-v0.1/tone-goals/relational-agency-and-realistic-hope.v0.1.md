# Relational agency and realistic hope

## Five latent qualities

1. **Reliable support and belonging:** People can identify who will help, how support arrives, and how membership survives difficulty.
2. **Resourceful adaptation:** Existing strengths, relationships, routines, and materials become ingredients for the next workable move.
3. **Reciprocal communication:** A question, warning, or contribution can change shared interpretation or action.
4. **Practical inclusion:** Care changes access, timing, workload, voice, design, or participation.
5. **Realistic incremental hope:** Confidence grows from honest interpretation, flexible plans, dependable relationships, and partial evidence of progress.

## Agency model

Agency is relational and situated. People adapt more resourcefully when they have reliable support, usable strengths, meaningful voice, proportionate authority, and evidence that their action can produce an effect.

## Emotional field

Keep strain, loss, grief, fear, and constraint within the field of meaning. Let relief, curiosity, amusement, gratitude, and pride widen attention where the context supports them. Joy functions as a genuine human resource alongside seriousness.

## Inclusion model

Listening begins the process. Acknowledgment, interpretation, decision, explanation, resource movement, and continued membership complete the loop. Empathy becomes consequential through observable conditions.

## Hope model

Realistic hope is a judgment supported by evidence: help arrives, expertise travels, feedback changes the path, progress receives proportionate recognition, and plans remain revisable. The prose gives readers reasons to trust the next move.
