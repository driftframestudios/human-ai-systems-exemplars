# Tone goals

Tone goals preserve felt qualities and instruction-bearing distinctions. Select the smallest combination that fits the artifact and its audience.

- [Warm Practical Register and Humane Structural Awareness](warm-practical-register-and-humane-structural-awareness.v0.1.md) — use as the integrative story-to-practice profile and register-analysis exemplar.
- [Register and Felt Temperature](register-and-felt-temperature.v0.1.md) — use for cadence, warmth, clarity, conceptual density, and reader effect.
- [Evidence and Structural Balance](evidence-and-structural-balance.v0.1.md) — use when research warrants, construct distinctions, power, workload, and institutional conditions need sustained attention.
- [Relational Agency and Realistic Hope](relational-agency-and-realistic-hope.v0.1.md) — use when support, communication, inclusion, situated agency, and credible progress carry the argument.

The integrative profile names rhetorical temperature and linguistic, narrative, organizational, epistemic, and critical registers. It also makes audience relationship, role-calibrated agency, and temporal and recovery rhythm explicit because those dimensions materially shape instructions.
