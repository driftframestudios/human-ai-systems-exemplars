# Semantic Attractor Design — Desired-State Guidance

This collection follows the stewarded guidance in Semantic Attractor Design — Negation-Aware Instructions. The repository companion lives at `field-guide/src/semantic-attractors/README.md`.

## Core move

State the desired condition directly. Describe what the interaction should feel like, what the artifact should preserve, what the system should optimize for, and what a collaborator should be able to observe.

## Strength ladder

1. A free-standing negation leaves the unwanted concept active and asks the reader or model to resolve its scope.
2. A familiar lexicalized antonym carries the distinction in one stable expression.
3. A positive constitutive predicate states what is true or what will remain true.

The third form provides the clearest attractor when the meaning supports it. Materially meaningful negation retains its place in safety, privacy, attribution, evidence, and definitional boundaries. A constructive replacement state accompanies each such boundary.

## Applied across this collection

- Tone goals describe the emotional and intellectual field to create.
- Instruction tranches name desired qualities and observable signals.
- Schemas describe extensible shared shapes.
- Reader guidance leads with the intended experience and practical use.
- Quoted titles, citations, and participant-supplied wording preserve their source form.

Expository prose receives a light treatment that protects natural cadence. The stricter desired-state practice belongs in prompts, instructions, metadata, and review language.

## Local coordination cue

**Target:** Tone, context, and evidence status stay intact across human–AI collaboration.

**Boundary:** Source access and participant attribution remain with their stewards.

**Replacement:** Collaborators use the tone goals, instruction tranches, references, contextual links, and schemas to recover meaning.

**Observe:** A reader can identify the intended tone, audience, evidence posture, relationships, and next useful move.
