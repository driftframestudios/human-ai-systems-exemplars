## Promotion route

one accepted state, keep the fingerprint independent of view, preserve its
source-owned state and recovery behavior, supply a readable no-JavaScript
default, and protect active-view byte agreement with focused tests. The current
28-source population meets that contract. A later manifest or source-population
change can begin a separately counted adoption from its own accepted owner.
