# Prompt Capsule inventory

- Inventory version: `0.1.0`
- Contract in active pilot: [`prompt-capsule/0.1.0`](0.1.0/prompt-capsule-contract.md)
- Classification basis: semantic ownership and projection role, not matching words

This inventory routes work to the source that can accept it. The observed
current population contains 28 source artifacts: 21 listed as current by the
Core manifest, four listed as current by the Narrative manifest, and three directly
maintained standalone artifacts. Strategy Canvas v0.2.4 is the one current
pilot adoption. The other 27 sources keep their accepted capsule behavior until
their owner has a receiving use and fitting migration evidence. A later
Narrative pilot can advance the adoption count while retaining this 28-source
denominator; its manifest row should name the accepted source rather than count
companion projections or generated archives as new sources.

## Promotion route

Another artifact joins this contract when its owner can produce both views from
one accepted state, keep the fingerprint independent of view, preserve its
import/export and recovery behavior, supply a readable no-JavaScript default,
and protect active-view byte agreement with focused tests. Until then, its
existing capsule remains the truthful current behavior.
