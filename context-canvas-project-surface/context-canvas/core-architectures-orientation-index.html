01 · Orientation resolver

A work strain becomes one bounded architecture question

Begin with what the team cannot yet see: a shared purpose, a threshold, a service boundary, tacit workflow, a usable signal, protected behavior, or an upstream source of strain. The family narrows the search; the architecture supplies a surface; the work produces a decision receipt and a route back when evidence changes.

03 · Measurement companion

HEART keeps a well-defined goal from collapsing into the easiest metric

A visitor can enter through a work strain, select the family that owns the missing decision, and then use HEART when the next question is evaluative: name the goal, describe the human or system signal that would indicate movement, and only then choose a metric. Interpretation, tolerance, and counter-signal remain beside the number so iteration stays accountable to the intended state rather than the proxy alone.

Debrief

Carry goal → signal → metric as one interpretation chain

Across HEART dimensions, each metric keeps its meaning, tolerance, and counter-signal available beside the value.

The Snag

Numbers are read faster than their conditions

A convenient count becomes the target, a proxy becomes success, and movement elsewhere in the system disappears from the review.

The Read

Measurement is a signal-to-metric calibration problem

The team needs to state what should be observed, how the metric represents it, which range matters, and what would contradict the reading.

The Move

Build the interpretation matrix before the dashboard

For each dimension, name the goal, signal, metric, interpretation, tolerance, and counter-signal; then decide which few measures deserve instrumentation.

The Cure

Measurement becomes evidence rather than verdict

Qualitative and quantitative signals can coordinate, proxy tradeoffs stay visible, and new evidence can revise the metric without silently changing the goal.

The Guidance

Ask which signal would change the next move

Instrument only after the population, decision, tolerance, and counter-signal make the measure interpretable.

Persona, aesthetic, and metric choice are operational variables

Prompt-Side Conditions treats persona and model aesthetic as part of the computation, not decoration. Persona-vector research shows that role framing can activate behaviorally meaningful directions (Anthropic, 2025 — persona vectors). HEART provides a guardrail: state the intended signal and counter-signal before asking an AI collaborator to optimize. Otherwise an easy metric can become the collaborator’s practical definition of success and narrow what it notices.

Use Verbalized Sampling before fixing instrumentation: produce several plausible signals and counter-signals, each with its key assumption, falsifier, and attentional frame. Select the metric only after comparing which candidate would support the real decision and which population it could systematically miss (Zhang et al., 2025 — Verbalized Sampling).

Inspect the paired HEART Chart

The companion makes the goal-to-signal-to-metric chain inspectable after its interpretive boundary is established. Use it as-is for one evaluative question; invert missing signals into a research and instrumentation plan; adapt dimensions to a domain; extend interpretation and tolerance with organization-owned definitions; connect accepted metrics to telemetry and review artifacts; or route counter-signals through a human-governed revision loop.

  1. Core Architectures Orientation Index — canonical seven-family taxonomy, twenty-architecture inventory, cognitive moves, entry landmarks, and recovery-routing posture.

Evidence Boundary. This is reusable machinery, not a Site Reading. The index gives a family and entry route; each architecture’s own source page carries its full relationship contract, recovery cues, artifact projection, semantic locality, and open questions. HEART’s five dimensions were validated within one company across twenty products, their application to agent trajectories has not been directly tested, the cited failure share comes from a single analyst source, and no study establishes that this exact goal → signal → metric ordering outperforms another sequence.
