Semantic Locality and Recovery

Preferred phrasing

  • What should become discoverable because this impact matters?
  • Which actor does this impact belong to?
  • What should become inspectable before we commit to an artifact form?

Recovery calibration

  • Strain cue: artifacts are proposed before actor impacts are visible.
  • Stabilizing read: the collaboration needs a visible effect-chain.
  • Restored state: goal-to-actor-to-impact-to-discoverable traceability.
