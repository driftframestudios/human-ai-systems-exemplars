Jobs-to-be-Done Treemap

A purpose-weighted job map that sizes jobs by relative consequence for a workflow, restored state, or collaboration purpose while preserving full data, SVG visualization, JSON/JSON-LD, semantic locality, and AI collaboration context.

v0.1 Pattern Fit and Behavior Protection Purpose-weighted treemap HTML + SVG + JSON-LD

Quick Use

Use this when: the team can name many jobs, tasks, responsibilities, workflows, or support needs, and needs to see which carry the most consequence for the overall purpose.

This restores: purpose-weighted attention.

Good next question: Which jobs carry the most workflow consequence, and what evidence supports their weight?

View State Principle

Stable IDs describe what a job is. View state describes where and whether it appears. Hide/display is focus state, not deletion or redaction.

This treemap inherits the chimeric artifact pattern: full text and full data remain preserved even when the visual surface is focused or compact.

Purpose and Focus View

Use one basis per view when possible, for example workflow consequence, governance responsibility, downstream risk, or qualitative participant emphasis.

Focus view: Jobs can be moved, hidden, and shown without changing their stable identity. Hidden jobs remain in JSON, JSON-LD, and the AI prompt’s hidden-scope note.

Source Note

      
This prototype adapts Jobs-to-be-Done as a Purpose-Weighted Job Map. The local use is not a complete JTBD research program. It sizes jobs by their relative consequence for the current workflow, purpose, or restored state, then routes high-consequence jobs into thresholds, examples, measurement, tests, patterns, service topology, or architecture views.

      
Product-market language is intentionally translated into actor, workflow, service, research, implementation, and collaboration context language.
