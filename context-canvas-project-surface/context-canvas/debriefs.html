Executive Summary · The system

Past work becomes specification by example

Context Canvas carries two complementary reading tracks. Debriefs reconstruct the people, operating conditions, consequential decisions, and implementation boundaries of the work. Executive summaries compress that evidence into a fast decision surface while keeping the route back to the fuller account visible.

Together, the collection functions as an enterprise learning system: one shared object can be followed through strategy, service design, architecture, quality, delivery, and return. The filters describe each reading’s available surfaces; the preview keeps its primary visual and next useful route close at hand.
