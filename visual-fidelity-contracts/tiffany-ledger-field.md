# Tiffany Ledger Field

## Purpose and fit

Tiffany Ledger Field is the About page's visual language for introducing a human practice, its enterprise consequences, and the instruments that make the practice inspectable. It balances a bright tiffany lead with cobalt structure, warm paper, dark iron-ore intervals, and solid color fields. The result is energetic enough to invite exploration and disciplined enough to carry staffing, quality, investment, and regulatory material.

Use it for the Context Canvas About surface and for compact orientation modules whose primary job is to answer: who is practicing, what changes, who carries the consequence, and where can the visitor inspect the work more deeply?

The current [About page](../context-canvas-project-surface/context-canvas/index.html) is the canonical implementation. [Twin Operating Folios](../collaboration/career-quality-visual-variants/index.html) records the comparison that established the Career at a Glance and Regulatory & Quality Expertise treatment. Embedded operational prototypes inherit [Cobalt Temporal Field](./cobalt-temporal-field.md) or [Cobalt Operating Field](./cobalt-operating-field.md) where time, state, and coordination become the primary decision dimensions.

## Compositional signatures

- Warm cream and paper form the default reading surface.
- Fine graph paper appears where relationships, prototypes, or operating ledgers are being inspected.
- Solid opaque fields interrupt the grid and mark changes in purpose.
- Dark iron-ore sections use chalk, cream, or paper copy; tiffany and acid-yellow emphasis remains sparse.
- Panels use `2px` ink frames and unblurred geometric shadows.
- Navigation cards use a cobalt frame, hard cobalt or ink shadow, an underlined title, plain paragraph copy, and a written arrow destination.
- Tiffany is strongest at entry and selection. Cobalt carries durable navigation and focus.
- Coral accompanies a written boundary, caveat, or consequence; it is never the only signal.
- The section before the footer may carry salmon as a warm completion cue. The footer settles into forest charcoal.

## Content anatomy

An About module carries the smallest useful combination of:

1. **Human purpose** — what the practice is trying to make possible.
2. **Operating object** — the decision, initiative, collaboration, or quality boundary in view.
3. **Population** — who acts, who reviews, who is affected, and who retains authority.
4. **Changed work** — the specific activity that becomes possible, unnecessary, or accountable.
5. **Evidence or currentness** — era, applicability, source posture, and what remains open.
6. **Route onward** — the relevant AI Systems instrument, Debrief, or full-size prototype.

Career and quality material uses a ledger rather than a row of decorative chips. The Twin Operating Folios keep the two records side by side when width permits and preserve two-up fact cells on narrow screens. Static fact cells look static: icons are decorative, labels are written, and button affordances belong only to actual controls.

## Type and density

- Use system sans for narrative and system mono for eyebrows, short labels, eras, and compact states.
- Follow the [shared reading measure](./README.md#shared-reading-measure): target approximately 50–66 characters per line and usually remain within 75.
- Use the shorter lead measure for larger introductory copy and a compact measure for standalone headings.
- Wide material becomes ledgers, columns, paired cards, or tables.
- Prose inside a wide ledger, card field, caption, route explanation, or evidence boundary returns to the shared measure.
- Reflow before reducing type. Supporting body text remains comfortably readable on narrow screens.
- Headings state a changed ability or enterprise consequence rather than naming a generic topic.

## Interaction and embedded instruments

Interactive modules remain visually distinct from surrounding prose. Their frames announce an inspectable field and keep their own state vocabulary. They meet the Cobalt interactive reference floor: explicit state and schema, semantic equivalents, substantive no-JavaScript content, named live regions, focus continuity, reduced motion, forced colors, and printable records.

Routes out of the About page state their destination and whether they open a new tab. An external or new-tab affordance uses icon, text, and accessible name together. Hover can add motion; meaning remains present without motion.

## Responsive, forced-color, and print behavior

- Paired operating folios sit side by side at wide and intermediate widths.
- Two-up fact ledgers remain two-up on narrow screens when their labels wrap comfortably; longer cases stack.
- Dense tables use labeled, keyboard-focusable local scrolling.
- Split backgrounds collapse into a deliberate source order rather than preserving an arbitrary left/right hierarchy.
- Forced-colors replaces fills with system colors and retains borders, written states, and focus outlines.
- Print removes embedded interactive frames, keeps their static equivalents and full-view links, neutralizes dark backgrounds, and prints both Career and Quality records.

## Evidence boundary

An About page can carry source-era or representative material with explicit technical standing. It names the material's currentness and applicability. Named receipts establish external validation, production shipment, or authorization. Positive description leads; consequential boundaries remain direct and paired with the state a visitor can rely on.

## Release checks

- Tiffany remains the lead accent and cobalt the durable structural color.
- Every colored region has a purpose visible in its copy or composition.
- Navigation items remain distinguishable from static ledgers.
- Career and Quality records preserve their complete shared content model.
- Abbreviations whose expansion helps evaluative readers use `<abbr title="…">`; ubiquitous interface terms remain plain.
- Prose width, narrow type size, local scrolling, focus, forced-colors, print, and no-JavaScript states are verified.
- Embedded prototype claims remain bounded by their own evidence and evaluation conditions.
