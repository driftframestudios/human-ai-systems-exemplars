# Zoetrope Preset Selection

Let visitors explore authored configurations by choosing a visible reel position
and seeing the resulting work immediately. The fixed housing, rotating center
and pressed choice make the setting easy to locate. Team arrangements, Change
viewpoints/frameworks and Quality preparation/recovery are current receivers.

**Standing:** review candidate `0.1.0`; ID `vfc:zoetrope-preset-selection`;
reviewed 7 November 2026. This pattern inherits
[Studio Rack Composition](README.md) and uses its Instrument Rack treatment.

## Fit and smallest useful dose

Use a reel for a bounded set of authored alternatives that produce a meaningful
visual variation. A labeled native selector remains a fitting neighbor for a
large or changing catalogue. One reel, its selected label and one nearby result
are a complete dose. A second reel earns its place when its distinct dimension
is named and both results remain understandable together.

The shared reel owns presentation and emits a selected ID. Its receiving
controller owns what that choice means. A viewpoint can change the reading;
a framework or arrangement can update a proposal. Those operations retain their
existing Apply and Undo boundaries. A visible animation never accepts work.

Teal and cobalt reel variants, authored option counts and paired configurations
may vary. The choice labels remain stationary while the center rotates. Selected
buttons keep their target geometry when pressed. A compact output repeats the
selected meaning; it does not add a competing instruction panel.

## Keyboard, motion and alternate presentation

Position buttons remain ordinary buttons. Arrow-key stepping follows the shared
reel's order and moves focus to the selected option; the center supplies a
single-point next-choice alternative. This is a visible button group, not an
undeclared tablist. Current callers supply unique, stable choice IDs.

Full-speed and Half-speed vary the decorative movement; Off and system reduction
settle directly on the same choice. A second reel may use a different settling
rhythm while its state remains immediately readable. Current quality extreme
settings can add decorative stutter. The nearby pressure reading identifies an
authored illustration; accepted price continues to come from people and work.

At ordinary mobile width, the receiving composition keeps both labels and
controls available. The rotor can simplify in forced colors while button borders,
focus and selected state retain their job. A receiver chooses its smallest useful
reel size through observed label and target geometry rather than shrinking text
to fit an arbitrary circle.
