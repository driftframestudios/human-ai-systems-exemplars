# Contextual Inspection & Recovery

Open the work behind a pad, change it in its rack, and return with the selected
record and unfinished entries intact. One canonical editor owns each proposal;
its placement follows the visitor's current working area.

## Recognizable controls

| Operation | Cue and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Inspect nearby detail | Caret and `aria-expanded` follow the visible receiver; pressed state identifies the selected object. |
| Change destination | Arrow and destination wording identify the move. |
| Create a record | Plus and Add wording identify a new accepted starting point; local controls then edit it. |
| Accept a prepared proposal | Raised Apply beside Close delegates to the canonical bottom action. A final confirmation carries acceptance meaning and its matching label. |
| Close or return | Ghost action retains unfinished entries and restores the initiating control or named working area. |
| Cancel edits | Ghost action restores accepted values. A record added earlier remains accepted. |
| Remove a plan/session record | Muted rose action opens a concise confirmation. Acceptance removes the record and dissolves its links together; amp Undo restores both. |
| Remove a working-session occurrence | Bottom-right rose action removes that session and remaps the remaining settings and placements with amp Undo; the plan's supporting relationships remain attached. |
| Undo | The stable native amp control reverses the latest accepted change through shared history. Its disabled state remains in place at zero. |

The [WAI Disclosure Pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/disclosure/)
is the semantic precedent for native activation, expanded state and right/down
cues. The [recorded control research](../../collaboration/native-investment-studio/rack-control-research.md)
explains the local distinction between selection, creation and acceptance.

## Acceptance and recovery

Creation and later editing are separate accepted-state decisions. A new person,
team, handoff or session enters shared history once with editing closed and a
clear New {item} N label. Creation inside a prepared form keeps that parent
form open and selects the new prerequisite there. Accepted bounded choices in
People—role facets, contribution category/receiver/decision owner, team membership
and supporting sessions—enter shared history directly and keep the initiating
control focused. Role pads keep their own visible state when acquiring an edit
owner. The person's unfinished name, rate and funded time remain recoverable;
role acceptance and person Apply retain their separate commands.
People participation retains open Card actions disclosures by person ID when
the roster refreshes. Selection and deselection preserve the clicked row's
usable viewport position, local horizontal scroll and keyboard focus; amp Undo
retains those disclosure choices while recovering accepted participation.
Contribution matrix checkboxes retain their local view while changing support.
When that acceptance advances a pending responsibility draft's base, its raw
entries remain available through the existing parking and Review owner. Row
headings alone toggle contribution inspection; their caret follows the visible
editor. Opening uses the shared smooth reveal, and closing retains entries and
returns focus locally without another scroll.
Team planning name, membership and reorder changes also accept immediately.
Session, person and handoff proposals, People team names and retained responsibility
drafts keep their Apply, Cancel and Close routes. Invalid person entries stay
visible in the draft until corrected or cancelled.
The shared top Apply mirrors its canonical submitter's label, disabled state
and validation. It invokes that submitter's existing operation and preserves
canonical validation and return-focus behavior. An editor that stays open can
retain header focus; a completed score edit closes its inspector and returns to
the originating marker. The bottom action row remains available. Removal stays
with its owning control; the loaded plan has a compact confirmation at the
transport's right edge.

Removing a plan record retains a short confirmation with its name and
Undo guidance. Acceptance clears that record from supporting-work selections,
period placements and stage membership. Its incident handoffs, dependencies and
anchored delays leave together. A decision with its own stage remains available;
a decision attached only to a removed handoff leaves with it. Proposed work
retains its entries and becomes independent of that removed decision. Supporting
teams, contributions, abilities, practices and quality/recovery records remain
available for new work. The canonical command accepts this complete change as
one history entry; amp Undo restores the original record and relationships.

Person and team removal keeps its existing relationship guard. A blocked
confirmation names each dependent record from its accepted name or versioned
catalogue: sessions, connections, contributions, abilities, practices and evidence
remain distinguishable. Arrangement, Change, Quality and horizon references keep
their review destinations. The shared reference renderer owns these descriptions;
the command owner retains deletion authority. Inspecting or cancelling a blocked
removal leaves accepted state and Undo unchanged.

Adding through a loaded plan's lane or track appends one accepted occurrence,
retaining the cassette and earlier placements. In All plans, explicit lane and
track New plan targets create distinct records while retaining that overview.
Shelf New plan deliberately loads the new cassette. Amp Undo reverses the accepted operation; ordinary
session settings and placement changes accept through Apply; explicit Working sessions Add/Remove actions accept directly with Undo.

Abilities also separates record creation from support assignment. Adding a person,
team or session accepts that record, then selects it within the current support
proposal. Apply accepts the relationship. Cancel restores the prior support
selection while retaining the added record; amp Undo can recover its creation.
Close and repeated selection park changed raw entries for reopening. The exact
initiating catalogue key or support cell receives focus, keeping the viewport in
place. Opening retains its smooth reveal. Carets and expanded state follow the
visible editor; the selected overview and accepted plan retain their own state.

Renaming a shared identity from Abilities follows the same ownership distinction.
Rename opens one local input with a concise plan-wide scope cue. Save or Enter
accepts the canonical label; Cancel or Escape returns to the current name. The
capability proposal keeps its raw entries and stable selected IDs while its
record labels refresh. Blank names remain editable with a short error; an
unchanged name leaves history intact. The local action receives focus on return,
and amp Undo recovers an accepted rename. This single-field receiver borrows the
record operation rather than the entire People form.

An editor switch parks unfinished work. A saved draft returns to a visible
receiver before focus enters its review control or requested field. Record,
rack and phase identity recover the initiating control after a board refresh.
A refused switch keeps the admitted record and visible editor aligned. The
canonical configure/status nodes move between their chapter home and the local
Coordination slot; their event listeners and draft ownership move with them.

A numbered session pad opens that session's name, duration, ordered participants,
funding, status and placement. Its target declares session scope and carries the
immutable work record that supplied its ordinal. The receiver validates that
record before changing editor ownership, local focus or return ancestry. An
incomplete or stale target preserves the current working area. Plan-default
navigation declares its own scope and stable work ID. Saved entries use the
canonical draft recovery route; returning presents that draft's current numbered
session. These semantic boundaries retain the existing palette, geometry and
motion. Its pressed state identifies only that occurrence;
the parent track remains a route to plan defaults. Working sessions pads switch
the same local owner. #910 supersedes the earlier shared-settings rule. Add/Remove
accept count changes with amp Undo after validating and accepting visible edits;
invalid entries remain available for repair. The enclosed duration module retains its separate
[layout contract](loaded-tape-duration-panel.md). Membership keeps cards in place;
explicit movement changes their order and retains focus on the same person.

Remove and existing relationship removal occupy the bottom-right action position.

new connections receive a concise default and appear in Delivery and Shared

Change and Quality use the same header and receiver scale, with their own
instrument palettes preserved. Related local views switch visibility while
their draft controls remain mounted. At laptop widths a supporting session's
duration module sits beside compact identity, participation and funding fields;
ordinary phone widths stack the modules. Selected keys and carets describe the
visible receiver. Close retains the draft and returns locally; Apply delegates
to the existing submitter. Primary reels, faders and reference displays retain
their established shape and motion.

The Change exact-settings disclosure follows the selected framework's component
names. A separate **Shared attention values** disclosure retains the underlying
cross-framework channels and explains their relationship to component emphasis.
Framework changes keep accepted and unfinished values available on return;
interpretive-lens choices retain their authored preset behavior. Both exact
routes remain inside the existing Change draft, Apply and amp Undo ownership
(#941).

[Ableton's Clip View](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/clip-view/)
provides the selected-record/detail precedent. The Studio adapts it as a
nonmodal working area with native form controls. A future blocking overlay

[WAI modal-dialog pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/).
