# Investment Master Shelf & Financial Reels

Let visitors load a financial comparison, turn a reel or dial, and see what
changes. A cassette identifies the comparison; a compact loaded rack connects
its assumptions, cash or capacity reading and alternative choices.

`vfc:investment-master-reels`; 9 November 2026, receiving

## Domain and financial portrait

The Domain reel moves among Internal, Customer, Commercial, Operations and
Planning. It loads the corresponding comparison type and preserves that deck's
settings. Loading a cassette also updates the Domain indication. The shared
reel keeps its usual native choices and focus behavior; chocolate and peach
color its housing and rotor. Its label and selected reading use warm paper against the dark rack, and its
fieldset is transparent. All choices retain the shared paper resting state and
teal pressed state regardless of wheel color.

To the right, an editorial companion explains what the domain helps a visitor
compare. A loaded deck presents an equal-angle rose with a semantic definition
list of named values. Each sector's **area** is proportional to the absolute
value, using a square-root radius. A caption states the unit and basis. The
series compares related quantities within that basis; several quantities can
overlap conceptually, so the exact labels carry their relationship.

| Domain | Portrait follows the loaded deck |
| --- | --- |
| Internal | Planned or known work, reserve, options and unassigned budget or shortfall, in USD |
| Customer | Price/delivery per team per quarter, or implementation setup/training across customer teams |
| Commercial | Retained opening-cohort revenue, expansion, new customers and reactivated revenue per quarter |
| Operations | Weekly time released and the remaining opportunity at full adoption, quarterly service cost, or people/capacity, according to the selected lens |
| Planning | Keep current, Pilot and Rollout compared through the selected NPV, IRR or payback reading |

Zero retains a zero reading. Unavailable results retain a written unavailable
state. Neither receives an invented visible magnitude. Negative quantities keep
their signed value and a heavier dashed petal edge. Color links each petal to
its exact reading; signs, labels and values remain independently readable.
Changing assumptions updates the portrait through the same financial projection
as the loaded deck.

When a domain has no deck, the companion presents a concise three-part flow
with a return path and a direct Add action. It reuses Coordination's filled-arrow
geometry, with a distinct rose return color. Creating the deck replaces that
explanation with the populated portrait. Removing and restoring a deck retains
the existing stable identity and amp-head Undo behavior.

Loading a different domain or cassette gives the portrait a short grow-and-settle
arrival and the loaded reel centers a brief spin. Current receiving motion is
`620ms × --studio-speed`; the rose settles with
`cubic-bezier(.18,.85,.3,1.12)`. Ordinary amount refresh updates the values in
place. Full-speed, Half-speed, Off and system reduction retain the shared motion
policy. Reduced-motion and forced-color visitors keep the exact readings.
