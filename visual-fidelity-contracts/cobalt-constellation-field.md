# Cobalt Constellation Field

## Purpose and fit

Cobalt Constellation Field extends [Cobalt Operating Field](./cobalt-operating-field.md) from one operating surface to a program of related capabilities. It helps an evaluator see how a shared object, multiple working populations, normal and recovery scenarios, repository references, and sustained investments form a coherent enterprise ability without turning the program into a maturity score or a decorative network.

Use it for LIMS/ELN programs, multi-service scientific platforms, distributed inspection systems, and other portfolios where several surfaces implement distinct parts of one decision contract. The [FMI LIMS/ELN program constellation](../context-canvas-project-surface/img/plot/fmi_program_constellation_map.svg) is the canonical static reference; its [comprehensive presentation](../collaboration/lims-eln-program-constellation/index.html) is the canonical inspectable implementation.

## Constellation anatomy

A useful constellation makes six layers readable:

1. **Shared object** — the sample, study rule, inspection record, case, or other durable object whose meaning coordinates the program.
2. **Capability families** — separately named abilities, not feature buckets.
3. **Scenarios** — normal work, exception, recovery, and re-entry conditions with named human authority.
4. **Executable references** — repository, schema, interface, runbook, prototype, or acceptance surface that makes the claim inspectable.
5. **Population consequences** — who acts, who is affected, who reviews, and who remains accountable.
6. **Investment** — the technical, quality, operational, and organizational work that sustains the program.

Hierarchy is explicit. A capability may connect to several scenarios, but every line carries a named relationship. Avoid an omnigraph: omit edges that do not change a visitor's decision.

## Visual grammar

- Cobalt is the connective spine; ink frames and hard shadows identify primary program regions.
- Warm paper preserves readable detail. Powder, sea glass, cream, blush, and acid separate roles only when a written label carries the same distinction.
- Use a stable top-level orientation, then capability clusters, scenario/acceptance bands, and an investment base.
- Integrated filled arrows carry direction. Thin undirected lines may show adjacency only.
- Repository and executable references use monospace labels and visible object types.
- A local magnifier may open one capability or recovery route without changing the meaning of the full constellation.

## Reading measure

Sustained explanatory prose follows the [shared reading measure](./README.md#shared-reading-measure): approximately 50–66 characters per line, usually remaining within 75. Introductions, capability explanations, scenario notes, repository annotations, captions, and acceptance guidance retain that measure.

The constellation uses its wider field for capability relationships, scenarios, references, populations, and investment. Each prose-bearing region remains locally readable, while the full constellation or semantic table owns any two-dimensional inspection space.

## Source and current-environment boundaries

Historical Site Reading material, later program specifications, and environment-observed implementation remain visibly distinct. A constellation can show their relationship while preserving separate labels, dates, and evidence posture. Simulated closure is never presented as environment-observed work. A repository surface proves only the behavior within its stated test and environment scope.

Every program claim closes with an acceptance or evidence need: scenario to exercise, artifact to inspect, population to involve, or current environment to observe. Use categorical states such as `established source`, `proposed contract`, `implemented reference`, and `open deployment question`; do not use readiness, confidence, or fidelity scores.

## Responsive and semantic expression

The static SVG has a decision-useful `<title>` and `<desc>`, semantic groups, integrated arrows, and no executable script. Pair it with a captioned table that maps capability → scenario → authority → reference → acceptance signal. Provide a full-size opening route.

At narrow widths, keep type readable and make the constellation locally horizontally inspectable, or present ordered capability cards plus the same semantic table. The page itself must not overflow. Forced colors preserve boundaries and written states; print neutralizes dark fields while retaining hierarchy and references.

An interactive constellation inherits the Cobalt interactive reference floor: stable IDs, deterministic state, closed schema, focus continuity, live status, substantive no-JavaScript equivalent, and the same content in its semantic register.

## Release checks

- The shared object and program boundary are explicit.
- Capability, scenario, repository, population, and investment layers are all represented.
- Historical, proposed, implemented, and environment-observed states remain distinct.
- Every directional edge has a written relationship and integrated arrow geometry.
- No score, rank, maturity ladder, or unearned equivalence appears.
- The static and semantic equivalents agree; full-size, narrow, print, forced-color, and no-script states are verified.
