# Evidence, authority, and publication

A public visual contract becomes trustworthy when meaning, schema, publication,
delivery, and receiving decisions remain distinct. One repository or person may
carry several roles; the record names each role so a later projection can retain
the boundary.

## Authority roles

| Role | Owns |
| --- | --- |
| Semantic owner | The meaning of the visual language and its invariants |
| Authored source | The exact repository file where that meaning is maintained |
| Schema owner | Structural validity and compatibility of the typed record |
| Example owner | Local behavior, code, CSS, data, and tests for a specimen |
| Publication owner | Acceptance of a public receiver and its version |
| Delivery operator | Retrieval, caching, headers, availability, and deployment |
| Receiving owner | Local adoption, mapping, authorization, domain validity, and operational receipt |

Stable identity and retrieval stay independent. A contract ID remains stable
while paths or hosts evolve. The public lineage record relates that identity to
its repository source and public route. Public artifacts carry public-safe
locators; private collaboration locators remain with their private owner.

## Evidence classes

- **Source inspection** establishes declared content and relationships in the
  files inspected.
- **Schema validation** establishes structural conformance for the exercised
  instances and validator configuration.
- **Unit behavior** establishes the assertions and failure cases exercised.
- **Browser observation** establishes rendered behavior for the named browser,
  version, viewport, media mode, and interaction.
- **Assistive-technology observation** establishes behavior for the named tool,
  browser, platform, and task.
- **Service observation** establishes the request and response boundary exercised.
- **Domain review** establishes scientific, regulatory, clinical, operational,
  or commercial meaning within its named authority.

A passing check leads with what it establishes. Adjacent claims receive their
own evidence. Silence leaves a claim open and routes it to the observation that
can establish it.

## Public projection

Public artifacts carry:

- descriptions maintained in the repository;
- stable contract IDs and public kindlight.systems routes;
- source and schema paths that resolve inside the repository;
- accepted standing and review dates;
- public-safe examples, synthetic fixtures, and evidence boundaries; and
- a re-entry event.

Private collaboration identities, page IDs, locators, comments, and access
coordinates stay with their private owner. A publication step adapts useful
meaning into the public contract and records the relationship in the authorized
private resolver.

## Promotion receipt

A reviewed promotion records the source revision, contract and schema versions,
tests run, public routes checked, receiving owner, evidence limit, and recovery
route. Exact-version mirrors preserve their identity-bearing bytes. A semantic
or byte change receives a new exact version or another reviewed revision under
the contract's compatibility policy.

## Review probe

1. **Question:** Which source defines meaning, which route publishes it, and
   which owner can accept its use?
2. **Mechanism:** Trace the contract ID through the source path, schema,
   examples, tests, public-lineage record, delivery route, and receiver.
3. **Observable evidence:** Every relation is typed; public paths resolve;
   private locators remain with their owner; the receipt names what passed.
4. **Instruction yield:** Repair the smallest broken relation or return the
   publication decision to its named owner.

## Re-entry

A changed meaning, schema, public route, example owner, publication standing,
delivery behavior, receiving authority, or evidence requirement reopens the
lineage map.
