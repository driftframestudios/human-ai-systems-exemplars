# Rose Quartz Editorial Field

## Purpose and fit

Rose Quartz Editorial Field is the pale narrative companion to [Cobalt Operating Field](./cobalt-operating-field.md). It carries the Solution side of a Debrief when the reader first needs to understand people, time, disagreement, reframing, and the object that made a decision discussable. The field is editorial rather than dashboard-like: warm paper, generous reading intervals, a small number of decisive diagrams, and no decorative grid competing with the story.

Use it when a source supplies a consequential narrative track and the implementation substrate follows as a distinct second movement. Do not impose it on a source-native single track, instrument reading, or chronology that does not exist. The [genomic viewer Context Tree presentation](../collaboration/context-tree-visual-variants/genomic-viewer-presentation.html) is the interactive reference; the genomic viewer Debrief is the canonical paired reading.

## Relationship to the two-track Debrief

A paired Debrief uses visual contrast to preserve two different questions:

- **Solution / Rose Quartz** — What did people encounter, what changed the account, and what became possible?
- **Implementation / dark Cobalt** — Which contracts, systems, states, populations, and investments make that ability dependable?

The transition is explicit in the heading, field, and copy. Color never substitutes for the written track label. A source-native temporal rail may use [Cobalt Temporal Field](./cobalt-temporal-field.md) within the pale movement when the original material supplies defensible dates or intervals.

## Reading measure

Rose Quartz prose follows the [shared reading measure](./README.md#shared-reading-measure), targeting approximately 50–66 characters per line and usually remaining within 75. Larger narrative leads use the shorter shared lead measure; standalone headings wrap on a compact measure that preserves their editorial cadence.

The pale field may span the page, while the narrative column remains measured. Context Trees, actor boards, temporal rails, and paired visuals receive wider regions for relationships; their captions, source distinctions, population accounts, and consequential decisions return to the reading column.

## Compositional signatures

- A quiet rose or paper field replaces the Cobalt graph grid.
- A small temporal rail or source-position label may sit near the top when earned.
- Context Tree, actor board, or shared-object diagrams use spacious columns and hard ink shadows.
- Text and diagrams alternate; neither becomes a wall of identically weighted cards.
- Cobalt joins the decisive relationship. Acid marks an explicit current or pivot state. Coral accompanies a written limit or consequence.
- A compact transfer panel names the changed ability and the implementation question it hands forward.

## Context Tree grammar

A Context Tree makes different accounts inspectable without forcing them into one vocabulary. Its useful minimum is:

1. the actor or population;
2. the situation and object they can name;
3. the question or consequence they carry;
4. the relationship to other accounts;
5. the decision or follow-on work that becomes possible; and
6. the evidence boundary that remains.

Columns, branches, or lenses remain source-local. They are not maturity levels. An interactive tree retains stable IDs, readable state and schema, a captioned table equivalent, substantive no-JavaScript content, focus continuity, live announcements, and reduced-motion, forced-color, and print treatments.

## Accessibility and evidence

Every figure carries a useful title, description, caption, and adjacent semantic equivalent where the relationship is consequential. State is expressed in words, border, and geometry as well as color. Dense diagrams have a full-size route and a locally scrollable narrow presentation.

Close propositions with a written Evidence Boundary. Distinguish observed source material, reconstructed dialogue, teaching examples, and proposed transfer. Name the next evaluation that can settle the boundary rather than converting uncertainty into a score.

## Release checks

- The pale movement answers a narrative or decision question supported by the source.
- Solution and Implementation are named in text when both exist.
- Time labels use only source-supported precision.
- Context Tree branches preserve population-local nouns and consequences.
- The field remains editorial: no gratuitous grid, dashboard density, or repeated card wall.
- Focus, semantic equivalents, print, forced colors, reduced motion, and narrow inspection are verified.
