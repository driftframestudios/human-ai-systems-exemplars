# Dual-Track Debrief Composition

## Purpose

Dual-Track Debrief Composition is a source-selection and page-composition contract for Context Canvas Debriefs. It joins existing visual languages without turning them into one universal page skin:

- [Rose Quartz Editorial Field](./rose-quartz-editorial-field.md) carries the human situation, consequential decisions, and solution shape.
- [Cobalt Operating Field](./cobalt-operating-field.md) carries implementation contracts, recoverable state, technical substrate, and sustained investment.
- [Cobalt Temporal Field](./cobalt-temporal-field.md) supplies early orientation only when source-supported time changes the reading.
- [Cobalt Constellation Field](./cobalt-constellation-field.md) supplies program-scale coordination only when several capabilities form one shared enterprise ability.

The contract answers a selection question before it answers a styling question: **which source-native movements does this Debrief actually contain?** A paired page names Solution and Implementation in words and changes field treatment at their handoff. A three-movement, comparative, temporal, or single-track reading keeps its own topology.

The [genomic viewer re-platforming Debrief](../context-canvas-project-surface/context-canvas/genomic-viewer-replatforming.html) is the maximal paired reference. The FMI clinical-trial and EHR series pages demonstrate lean paired adaptations. Humatics demonstrates the explicit non-paired, three-movement variant.

## Governing posture

The composition reveals distinctions that help a visitor interpret the work:

1. what people encountered and which decision shape became useful;
2. what the implementation had to preserve, recover, or make inspectable;
3. what outcome actually occurred;
4. which evidence supports the account and which boundary remains; and
5. which populations and investments keep the ability dependable.

Track color, page length, diagram count, and card density follow those questions. They never substitute for them. A field labelled `Solution architecture` remains identifiable in monochrome. An outcome labelled `Partial`, `Pivoted`, `Successful`, or `Failed` remains legible without its accent. An Evidence Boundary states what the page can and cannot establish.

## Source-first selection

Read the canonical source, its Docket or coordinator, and the existing page before choosing a composition. Record:

- source-native movement names;
- whether the account is delivered history, participant recollection, synthetic reconstruction, current reference, or proposal;
- outcome type in the source's own terms;
- dates, ranges, and temporal qualifiers that change interpretation;
- diagrams already earned by the material;
- populations carrying the decision or consequence; and
- evidence or disclosure boundaries that must remain visible.

Use the smallest adequate composition.

| Source structure | Composition | Field posture | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full paired account with deep narrative and implementation evidence | **Maximal paired** | Several Rose Quartz movements followed by several dark Cobalt movements | Genomic Viewer; Field Inspection |
| Paired account with complete teaching spine but lower visual density | **Lean paired** | One Rose solution field and one Cobalt implementation field, each with written label and boundary | FMI Clinical Trial Coordination; EHR SR1–SR5 |
| Multi-capability program organized around one shared object | **Program paired / constellation** | Rose or paper solution orientation plus Cobalt Constellation implementation surface | FMI LIMS/ELN |
| Three source-native movements | **Three-movement** | Distinct Narrative, Solution, and Implementation fields | Humatics |
| Equal cases on one axis plus a temporal return | **Comparative / temporal** | Equal comparison field and Cobalt Temporal rail | Three-Way Comparative & The Return |
| One substrate, instrument, research, or method reading | **Single-field** | Cobalt Evidence, Cobalt Operating Field, or source-native editorial field | Initiative Costing; NEG Atlas; Prompt-Side Practice |

Do not add a second track merely because the page is a Debrief. Do not split Narrative from Solution when the source treats the narrative as the solution-shape account. Do not add a constellation when one operating object and one delivery path are sufficient.

## Paired semantic anatomy

A lean or maximal paired Debrief reads in this order:

1. **Orientation** — title, era, source posture, central architectural reading, and useful scan fields.
2. **Outcome state** — a compact written statement that names the result without treating it as a score.
3. **Reading route** — native links to each track and to consequential supporting surfaces.
4. **Solution movement** — written track label, source-led problem and decision shape, one or more decisive visuals, and a Rose Evidence Boundary.
5. **Implementation movement** — written track label, system and contract mechanics, recovery or assurance state, and a Cobalt Evidence Boundary.
6. **Changed work and investment** — the populations, work shifts, residual responsibility, and sustained capability.
7. **Source basis** — citations, disclosure posture, and qualifications.

Maximal pages may separate each five-beat section into its own field. Lean pages keep the complete five-beat spine within one field per track. Compression removes repetition and card walls; it does not remove the outcome, evidence boundary, or semantic equivalent.

## Written track labels

Movement labels and main headings share a full-width header outside the
fragmented prose body. A beat heading opens its reading region above both
columns, while ordinary internal headings keep their local rhythm. The
`.debrief-movement`, `.debrief-movement__header`, and `.debrief-reading-flow`
classes express these relationships. Each beat heading and full-width
evidence field stands between continuous `.prose-flow` runs, preserving
source order in the rendered reading. The shared reading component owns
header containment and beat spacing; core foundation owns the responsive
columns within each run. Palette, reading measure, and route topology retain
their existing owners.

Every movement begins with a compact label before its main heading. A useful label contains:

- ordinal or movement name;
- architecture type;
- visual field name or decision function; and
- one short description of what the visitor should inspect.

Examples:

- `TRACK I · SOLUTION ARCHITECTURE · ROSE QUARTZ EDITORIAL FIELD`
- `TRACK II · IMPLEMENTATION ARCHITECTURE · COBALT OPERATING FIELD`
- `MOVEMENT I · NARRATIVE ARCHITECTURE · COLLABORATION CHANGES ALTITUDE`

The ordinal aids navigation; it is not rank. The architecture type and inspectable question carry the meaning.

## Outcome state

Outcome is categorical, source-native, and written near the top of the reading. Useful states include:

- **Partial** — an ability landed while a named dependency or residual condition remained.
- **Pivoted** — the initial direction was retired and a different architecture became the durable result.
- **Successful with a named near-miss** — the outcome landed and the near-miss remains part of the teaching value.
- **Failed** — technically correct work did not produce the intended operating or human effect.

State language includes the consequential clause: `Failed — correct delivery did not change the receiving workflow.` Color may vary by state, but a bold word, border geometry, and explanatory sentence carry the distinction.

Outcome is never a maturity level, confidence score, or verdict on people. Failure and partial results remain first-class evidence.

## Evidence Boundary

Each architecture movement closes, or is immediately followed, by a written Evidence Boundary. It names:

1. the evidence posture: delivered history, attributed account, synthetic reconstruction, contemporary reference, or proposed transfer;
2. what the source supports;
3. what the page does not establish;
4. any vendor, organization, era, or environment boundary; and
5. the observation or artifact that could settle an open claim.

The boundary uses a coral rail, written `EVIDENCE BOUNDARY` label, and bordered surface. The dark-field version uses a warm paper card on Cobalt. It is not a warning box and does not collapse uncertainty into a numeric score.

For synthetic EHR readings, every page states the reconstruction posture near orientation and repeats the movement-specific implication at the boundaries. For participant accounts, dialogue and outcome attribution remain qualified where the distinction changes interpretation.

## Field composition

### Solution / Rose Quartz

- Use warm rose, paper, and quiet-paper fields.
- Let prose establish people, object names, disagreement, and the decision shape before diagrams become dense.
- Use Cobalt for decisive connective relationships and coral for a written consequential boundary.
- Give figures a warm field, ink outline, hard shadow, full-size opening route, useful `alt`, and caption.
- Keep one major visual per consequential decision when possible.

### Implementation / dark Cobalt

- Use deep Cobalt or iron as the outer field with light text.
- Keep tables, system cards, code, and evidence surfaces on warm light panels; do not reverse dense table text onto Cobalt.
- Name states, identifiers, target systems, recovery destinations, and residual work.
- Use integrated arrow shapes for directional system movement.
- End with the operational ability and sustained investment rather than a technology inventory.

### Supporting fields

Changed work, population cartography, and sources may return to warm paper. A compact Cobalt band may connect the two tracks, but supporting content does not need to inherit either full field merely for visual continuity.

## Reading measure

Both tracks inherit the [shared reading measure](./README.md#shared-reading-measure), targeting approximately 50–66 characters per line and usually remaining within 75. Solution and Implementation may occupy parallel fields, while each paragraph, list item, caption, source note, and Evidence Boundary remains locally readable.

Track width expresses parallel responsibility rather than longer lines. On a wide display, explicit paired regions support comparison; on a narrower display, the same regions follow a deliberate source order. Tables, system diagrams, and temporal rails retain local two-dimensional space while their surrounding interpretation returns to the prose measure.

## Temporal and constellation extensions

Add [Cobalt Temporal Field](./cobalt-temporal-field.md) near orientation when time changes interpretation: a return years later, an external gate running on another calendar, a source-supported near-miss interval, or a historical engagement compared with a contemporary reference. Preserve source precision. An approximate four-quarter reconstruction stays approximate; it does not become a set of invented dates.

Add [Cobalt Constellation Field](./cobalt-constellation-field.md) only when a visitor needs to inspect several capability families, shared scenarios, repository references, populations, and investments as one program. A two-target integration is a fan-out or comparison, not automatically a constellation.

## Visual and semantic equivalents

Every consequential SVG includes:

- one decision-useful `<title>` and `<desc>`;
- semantic groups with local titles;
- written state and relationship labels;
- integrated directional arrows where direction is claimed;
- a static base state and no executable script;
- color paired with geometry and words;
- a full-size opening route; and
- adjacent prose, caption, or a semantic table carrying the essential relationship.

Distinct readings receive distinct diagrams when reusing an atlas image would erase the page's actual decision. For example:

- SR1 centers the order → specimen → result → finding → patient contract, the EMPI match membrane, and the parallel vendor gate.
- SR2 centers one canonical finding, two identity membranes, two emissions, and two independent receiving calendars.
- SR3 may use the three-front-door atlas because distribution across three targets is the decision.

## Responsive, forced-color, reduced-motion, and print behavior

- Reflow before reducing type. Preserve headings, labels, and card padding at narrow widths.
- Keep wide tables in named, keyboard-focusable scroll regions; the page itself does not overflow.
- A dark implementation field becomes a light print field with black text and retained borders.
- Forced colors remove hard shadows and preserve track, outcome, current-route, and Evidence Boundary distinctions through borders and written labels.
- Reduced motion freezes any supplementary emphasis; all state remains present without motion.
- Print preserves both tracks, outcomes, evidence boundaries, captions, and source basis. Navigation may simplify, but the reading remains complete.

## CSS and repository posture

The family now exposes five readable ownership layers. `styles/components/debrief-reading.css` carries shared reading-route and source-basis components, full-width movement headers, and heading-first reading regions. `styles/routes/debriefs.css` carries the catalog experience, including its Debrief-card badges and metadata. `styles/routes/three-movement-debrief.css` carries the source-native three-movement route through neutral structural names. `styles/routes/genomic-viewer.css` carries the maximal paired Rose Quartz and dark Cobalt route, including its narrow, forced-color, print, and shared-component cascade seams. `styles/contracts/debrief-composition.css` remains the reusable movement, field, handoff, outcome, and Evidence Boundary implementation scoped under `.debrief-composition-page`.

Pages link the layers they use in explicit cascade order: `expanded.css`, the shared reading component, an applicable route bundle, `canvas-shell.css`, and then `styles/contracts/debrief-composition.css` for composed readings. The maximal paired reference uses `expanded.css → debrief-reading.css → genomic-viewer.css → canvas-shell.css`. A non-paired page may use the label and boundary components without adopting paired Rose/Cobalt fields. Each route retains its own palette, rhythm, density, topology, and source-earned experiments.

Base pages remain semantic HTML, CSS, and SVG. No framework or page-level interaction is required for the composition. Embedded prototypes may use small, inspectable vanilla JavaScript when behavior itself is part of the reading.

## Release checks

- Movement count and names follow the source rather than the template.
- Solution, Implementation, and any third movement are named in text.
- Outcome state appears near orientation and matches the source basis.
- Every movement has a useful Evidence Boundary.
- Rose and Cobalt fields remain legible without color.
- Dark fields keep dense tables and code on light readable surfaces.
- Temporal precision never exceeds the source.
- Reused visuals preserve the local decision; otherwise a distinct visual is authored.
- SVG titles, descriptions, groups, IDs, arrows, captions, and full-size paths validate.
- Narrow, wide, print, forced-color, reduced-motion, keyboard, and no-JavaScript readings remain complete.
- Source citations, disclosure posture, and vendor qualifications are present wherever they change interpretation.
- No score, maturity ladder, universal two-track claim, or unsupported causal arrow is introduced.
