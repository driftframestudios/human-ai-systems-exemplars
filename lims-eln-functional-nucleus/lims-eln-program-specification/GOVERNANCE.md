# Decision Rights

The program repository coordinates intent while domain authority remains with
the people accountable for each meaning:

- Scientific owners define specimen, lineage, measurement, review, and release
  semantics.
- Quality owners approve the assurance strategy, deviation disposition,
  procedural readiness, and residual-risk record.
- Regulatory owners determine applicability for the named intended use and
  jurisdiction.
- Security and Privacy owners approve data classification, identities,
  telemetry treatment, retention, and incident posture.
- Platform and Operations owners approve reliability, recovery, support, and
  environment operation.
- Product and business owners accept material workflow, adoption, and
  continuity effects.
- Engineering owners implement and review code, interfaces, and migrations.

Pull-request approval records engineering governance. Accountable acceptance
uses the role-bound receipt contract and its approved signature system.

## Bounded caller authority

Humans, services, workflows, and AI systems call only released service-owned
operations. Authentication establishes caller identity; service policy
establishes authority, tenant scope, resource scope, budgets, approval posture,
effect ceiling, audit, and recovery.

A task-scoped AI identity can receive read and narrow idempotent-write
operations through an explicit allowlist. Approval-gated broad writes bind an
unexpired human approval to the canonical request. Scientific, Quality,
Regulatory, Security, Operations, and business acceptance remains with the
accountable human roles above.

## Simulated acceptance

Role-bound synthetic decision records can complete the full acceptance shape
for a selected future reality. Those records carry `evidence_mode: simulated`
and remain bound to their declared artifact role. Program design records carry
`authority: advisory_design_only`. A canonical executed closure can be
definitive within its selected future reality while
`production_authorization_effect: none` preserves the boundary to production.
Each corresponding receipt preserves a separate future observed state whose
advancement is governed by its own evidence and accountable human decisions.

## Forecast integrity

A forecast release precedes the corresponding actual observation. Its selected
option, probability, assumptions, falsifiers, and expected effects remain
unchanged. Subsequent learning creates a successor forecast.

## Comparative independence

The actual and simulated implementations share released specifications,
scenario identifiers, and fixture contracts. Their runtime source histories
remain independent. The comparator owns normalization and classification, with
independent review for fixture and expected-result changes.

## Release settlement

A candidate carries explicit zero provenance, a settling Coordination Horizon
item, and the open promotion receipt
`RCP-PROGRAM-PROMOTION-003`. A released record carries material commit and tree
coordinates, observed ruleset and CODEOWNER evidence, completed promotion
work, local validation evidence, and the hosted contexts active for that
release. The current `$0` resting state carries an empty hosted-context list
while protected review and history controls remain active.

Release and hotfix validation resolves every external repository from its
nonzero 40-hex lock coordinate before checkout. A fine-grained
`CONSTELLATION_READ_TOKEN` repository secret supplies read access to the four
private sibling repositories. The token receives repository-contents read
access only and follows the repository secret-rotation policy.

The pinned governance validator applies the released Machine Node, first
receipt, and Coordination Horizon schemas to this repository. The settlement
gate also compares canonical bundle bytes, simulation result content,
governance closure, and aggregate artifact digests across the program,
simulation, and comparison repositories.
