# Explore the Native Investment Studio

[Run the Studio and try your first edit](../README.md#run-the-studio-locally).
The example starts with a populated plan so you can see how people, sessions
and funding affect one another.

## Choose a chapter

| Chapter | Try this |
| --- | --- |
| **Coordination** | Select a plan cassette to see its sessions in the sprint board and tracks. Select **All plans** to see the complete arrangement. Open a sprint for its weeks or choose **Plan overview** to return. **Try a different example** loads another arrangement, including **AI Enablement** and **AI Experience**. One **Undo** restores the previous plan. |
| **People** | Select a person to inspect their rate, funded time and role. Toggle a participation checkbox to change their assignment to a session. Open a contribution to find the person who receives the work and the person who owns the decision. |
| **Abilities** | Open a support cell to review the people, teams and sessions that help deliver an ability. **Apply** accepts the proposal. **Rename** edits the shared name of a referenced person or team. |
| **Change** | Turn a method reel and open one of its actions to inspect the proposed work and the people who carry it. |
| **Quality & recovery** | Open a quality or recovery action to inspect who helps, what they review and what the plan records. |
| **Investment** | Use **Plan investment** to inspect the cost of accepted work or open a financial deck to explore a comparison. Use **Compare a change** to capture a baseline and **Keep a plan** for file exchange and reports. |

In Coordination, a cassette is a repeatable plan, and each session is one
occurrence. **Add session** repeats the loaded plan. **New plan** creates
separate work. Session cards open individual settings, and a track name opens
the plan defaults. Sessions with custom settings keep those settings when you
edit the defaults.

## Edit, recover and carry a plan forward

- **Direct choices** such as participation checkboxes update the accepted plan
  immediately. Use **Undo** in the Studio's top panel to recover an accepted change.
- **Apply changes** accepts the editor's proposal. **Close** keeps unfinished
  entries in the open page. Find them under **Saved edits** in the top panel.
  **Cancel changes** returns the editor to its accepted values.
- **Download current plan** under **Investment → Keep a plan** saves the
  accepted plan as JSON. Finish applying the edits you want to carry forward
  before downloading. The current page retains unfinished edits, the baseline,
  and Undo history until reload or close.
- To resume from a file, choose **Investment → Keep a plan**, open the plan
  file, and inspect the preview. **Use this plan** replaces the accepted plan
  in the current page. **Undo** restores the preceding plan. A rejected file
  leaves the current plan available.

The Studio's **Operating manual** explains these controls beside the plan.
The [experience journal](../experience-journal.md) explains the interaction
decisions, and the [visual contracts](../../../visual-fidelity-contracts/studio-rack/README.md)
show the conventions for selectors, editors and recovery.
