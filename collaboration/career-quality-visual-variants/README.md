# Career + Quality visual treatment comparison

This directory preserves the design comparison that led to the production **Twin Operating Folios** treatment on the Context Canvas About page.

## Artifacts

- [`index.html`](./index.html) — five complete renderings of one Career and Quality record.
- [`threadkeeping/about-career-quality-treatment-comparison.md`](../../threadkeeping/about-career-quality-treatment-comparison.md) — selection history, fidelity invariants, unresolved source questions, and the production handoff.
- [`context-canvas-project-surface/context-canvas/index.html`](../../context-canvas-project-surface/context-canvas/index.html) — the production About surface receiving the selected treatment.

## Shared content model

The comparison keeps one semantic source for two records:

- 16 operating facts;
- 6 role records;
- 11 consequential situation records; and
- 4 Quality qualification states.

Every situation carries its situation, mechanism, changed work, boundary, phase, domain, and qualification. The comparison checks that all five treatments retain the same record before they are compared aesthetically.

## Five transferable treatments

1. **Twin Operating Folios** — simultaneous, equal-standing comparison. Selected for the About page.
2. **Rotating Dossier** — focused reading when vertical space is scarce and switching is materially useful.
3. **Career × Quality Translation Ledger** — phase-aligned comparison for readers tracing changed work across two disciplines.
4. **Two-Lens Service Blueprint** — a shared lifecycle with parallel evidence lanes and expandable mechanisms.
5. **Evidence Case Files** — compact first scan with deliberate disclosure of deeper evidence.

These are compositional patterns, not page templates. Reuse the cognitive move and accessibility behavior; regenerate the vocabulary, hierarchy, evidence, and visual balance from the receiving context.

## Transfer rules

- Keep static facts semantic and visibly non-interactive.
- Preserve source nouns, currentness, applicability, and accountable decision boundaries.
- Give parallel records equal semantic standing without implying equivalence.
- Reflow before reducing type; dense wide structures use labeled local scrolling.
- Pair color with written labels, geometry, or icons.
- Treat fact icons as decorative wayfinding: pair each cell with a context-specific inline SVG while its written term carries the meaning.
- Expand consequential, domain-specific initialisms with semantic `<abbr>` markup; leave common working vocabulary unburdened and keep critical meaning in the surrounding prose.
- Maintain a complete no-JavaScript and print reading when interaction is present.
- Treat the comparison's content-carriage receipt as an evaluation aid; it does not travel into production pages.

The five treatments draw from the [operating-field composition guide](../../visual-fidelity-contracts/cobalt-operating-field.md). Side-by-side, focused-record, lifecycle, expandable-service, and compact-case arrangements let each reading task choose a useful evidence density without changing the underlying records.
