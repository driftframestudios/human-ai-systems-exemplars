# Semantic table design

## What

A source-owned table contract that preserves the reader's operation, native
header relationships, precise values and locally complete meaning across visual,
interactive and extracted forms.

## When

A table's content, structure, layout, supplemental labels, generated representation
or receiving use is being assessed or changed. Direct reasoning remains sufficient
for a small correction whose meaning and owner are already established.

## Boundaries

Authored semantics and the owning generator establish meaning. Native HTML
establishes baseline table relationships; custom attributes serve named receivers.
Plain-word label conventions are design choices, with literal names, language,
units and technical notation retaining their identity. Geometry, extraction and
source parity establish separate claims. Accessibility conformance, arbitrary
model or ATS ingestion, parser adoption and publication retain their own evidence
and authority.

## Result

A bounded table-family disposition naming the reader job, semantic and source
owners, header relationships, field roles, supplemental context, scroll boundary,
affected states, fitting observations and any unresolved interpretation.

## Fit and attention

- **Useful when:** A reader must look up, compare, enter or reconcile precise values, or a receiving tool encounters records apart from their page.
- **Poor fit when:** Content is primarily sequential prose, a chart alone serves the requested pattern-reading task, or a read-only inventory needs no design change.
- **Smallest useful dose:** One affected table or shared family, its reader operation and meaning owner, and the observation that can change the current decision.
- **Foregrounds:** Relationships between headers and values, intact qualifications, complete record context and the actual width available to the reader.
- **May under-attend:** Correct source associations can coexist with difficult interaction, and good geometry can coexist with incorrect meaning. Each consequential receiving behavior needs its own observation.
- **Material result:** A usable comparison or record, or an explicit source-owner question that prevents an ambiguous transformation from being published.
- **Interactions:** Source-authority mapping traces generators and projections; embedded-prototype layout owns parent/child pressure; cost analysis owns traversal and expansion; visual verification selects fitting rendered evidence.
- **Rest or release when:** The changed boundary has adequate evidence and an accountable receiver, or a specific uncertainty has a recovery route. A new state or owner can reopen the fitting scope.

## Rules

### Rule: The reader operation selects the representation

Lookup, cross-row comparison, entry, reconciliation and evidence inspection have
different reading needs. A table serves two-dimensional relationships or precise
values; a chart may accompany it for patterns. Ordering, grouping and selective
emphasis support the named operation within the existing visual language. A
conversion to cards preserves a comparison route when comparison remains a task.

### Rule: Semantic ownership precedes editing

The collaborator traces authored markup, shared semantic descriptors, generators,
runtime data, styles, maintained projections and applicable export/print output.
A generated copy returns to its canonical owner. An authored patch preserves its
unowned source. Historical copies retain their comparison role. One reviewed
wording decision can serve several renderers without giving a display normalizer
authority over every string they receive.

### Rule: Header topology is explicit

Each data field has an understood relationship to its column, row and applicable
group headers. Native `th`, fitting `scope` and, where needed, explicit header
associations carry those relationships. Identity follows the semantic contract,
which may use an offset header or several fields. Complex spans and repeated
headings receive a relationship map or a simpler design before a flat extractor
assumes one positional label per cell.

### Rule: Record shape remains meaningful

A declared simple table validates every record's required fields, including a row
left with headers only after a data cell disappears. A present blank value differs
from a structurally absent field. Group headings, totals and empty-state rows
retain their declared roles. Ambiguous structure returns to its source owner
before derived labels can make a partial record appear complete.

### Rule: Supplemental records carry the context their receiver needs

A detached record retains topic, record identity, field meaning, value and
applicable units, status, period, conditions and evidence qualifications. Source
or stable identifiers travel when the receiver uses them. Custom `data-label`
values supplement this contract; native relationships and visible meaning remain
usable when those attributes are ignored. Added repetition earns its place by
restoring context that extraction would otherwise lose.

### Rule: Plain-word labels preserve meaning and literal data

Supplemental wording derives from the same semantic owner as the visible label.
A known decorative separator or directional shorthand can receive ordinary words
or simple punctuation when its interpretation is clear. Accented names, symbols
with technical meaning, units, identifiers and user-entered records retain their
literal content. Normalization acts on owned semantic components before the
existing context-appropriate escaper; a general Unicode deletion or transliteration
rule has a different, ungranted scope.

### Rule: Values keep their distinctions

Zero, blank, unavailable, invalid, inapplicable and uncertainty retain the source
contract's distinct meanings. Signs, precision, units, denominators and periods
stay interpretable. Qualifiers remain adjacent in the visible record and available
to its named extractor. Identity, numeric, status, prose and code/identifier
columns receive fitting reading and wrapping choices.

### Rule: Comparison geometry belongs to its actual container

Necessary two-dimensional width can live inside a named, keyboard-reachable local
scroll region while surrounding prose and controls reflow. Column measures protect
individual values and text, including the final column. Embedded work uses the
child's measured available width as well as standalone controls; a nominal outer
viewport alone leaves the child constraint unspecified.

### Rule: Derived work is bounded before expansion

Transformation admission accounts for source size, structure, repeated labels,
escaping and total output before materializing large derived collections.
Repeated caption copies and scanner suffix copies have their own cost. Raw code
and unselected owners retain their boundaries. Unsupported syntax, source drift
and missing semantic fields receive explicit outcomes rather than partial
publication of a supposedly complete table.

### Rule: Observation follows the changed boundary

The smallest sufficient evidence distinguishes meaning, shape, native structure,
source preservation, generated/extracted parity, rendered geometry and interaction.
A metadata-only wording change begins with its label consumers and source or
generator contracts. A layout, state or accessible-relationship change selects
the relevant rendered and interaction cases. Existing coverage remains reusable
only within its actual identity and scope; a browser pass is neither a universal
requirement for every edit nor a substitute for semantic evidence.

## Decisive scenarios

### Scenario: A row leaves its page

- **Given:** A row contains a numeric value whose unit and period are stated only above the table.
- **When:** A named receiver exports that row independently.
- **Then:** The exported record carries the applicable topic, identity, field, unit, period and qualification, and its test checks the detached meaning.

### Scenario: A flat label rule meets grouped headers

- **Given:** Two column groups each contain a field named `Actual`.
- **When:** A simple projector cannot retain their group distinctions.
- **Then:** The source owner supplies an explicit association/record contract or simplifies the table; the unresolved projection remains a visible review disposition.

## Completion and re-entry

The receiver can identify the owner, understand the comparison or detached
record, operate the affected controls, and locate evidence for the changed
boundary. Any unsupported shape or unobserved behavior has a named receiver and
re-entry condition. A new consumer, vocabulary ambiguity, projection drift,
changed parent width or missing field reopens the smallest fitting scope.
