# Context Canvas surface reading

## Contract

- **What:** A source-grounded model of one page or route family.
- **When:** Assessment, planning, or material visitor-surface change begins.
- **Boundaries:** Page meaning remains rooted in authored source, evidence boundaries, and human authority. Visual variation remains available within the wider grammar.
- **Result:** Route standing, visitor ability, evidence boundary, runtime owners, visual fingerprint, and affected family.

## Rules

### Rule: The page is design input

Prose, diagrams, composition, and interaction patterns can shape later repository work. Page content is therefore read as behavior and design evidence rather than interchangeable copy.

### Rule: Contracts orient; routes compose

Visual Fidelity Contracts stabilize semantic grammar and canonical references. Route families retain authority over palette, rhythm, density, spatial arrangement, delivery mechanism, and source-earned experiments.

### Rule: Runtime ownership stays explicit

HTML owns meaning and reading order. CSS owns presentation and modes. Progressive JavaScript owns bounded enhancement. Embedded systems and delivery boundaries retain separate authority.

### Rule: Reading depth follows decision leverage

A catalog or full-family question begins with aggregate route, class, asset, iframe, stylesheet, and runtime inventory. Complete source reading then deepens around exceptions, affected consumers, and relationships capable of changing the decision. A material change to one page still receives its complete shaping source.

### Rule: Semantic and rendered observations stay reconcilable

Document meaning, accessibility-tree or DOM representation, rendered visibility, geometry, and screenshot content are distinct observation channels. Agreement in one channel leaves the others open. When an AI-visitor claim enters, the reading identifies the agent's actual channel and version, compares it with the corresponding human-visible task, and treats benchmark architecture as a hypothesis source until a local task supplies behavior evidence.

### Rule: Available width serves content relationships

A wide-region investigation distinguishes readable prose measure from the container's distribution of content. The reading identifies continuous narrative, comparable records, instructions, code, and onward links before choosing their arrangement. It measures usable space after fixed neighbors and padding, then asks which units can stand beside one another while preserving source order and their shared heading. Independent groups may form a grid; continuous prose retains its reading sequence and a comfortable line length. Short orientation and intentional negative space can remain as authored. A contained scrollbar establishes reachability; useful distribution requires its own visitor-task observation.

## Operating sequence

1. **Orientation:** The reading begins at `context-canvas-project-surface/context-canvas/README.md`.
2. **Complete source:** One target page receives each materially shaping stylesheet, script, iframe, SVG, image, manifest, and data source. Catalog-scale work uses inventory to locate the exceptions and affected consumers that receive this depth.
3. **Grammar:** The relevant record under `visual-fidelity-contracts/` and one canonical sibling supply family relationships when those relationships matter.
4. **Delivery context:** `context-canvas-project-surface/context-canvas/grammar-family.html` supplies repository context when Cloudflare, contextual composition, coordinated rooms, durable undertakings, or visitor-owned surfaces enter the question.
5. **Surface map:** The reading records standing, visitor ability, evidence boundary, composition fingerprint, runtime owners, and family relationships.
6. **Consequence map:** The resulting map names the improved visitor ability, source owner, affected family, smallest useful observation, and human-held decision authority.

## Representative scenarios

### Scenario: A composed Debrief receives a visual change

- Given a Debrief carries shared composition grammar and a route-specific fingerprint
- When a visual change is assessed
- Then shared contract relationships and route-local expression are mapped separately
- And the comparison preserves meaningful variation while surfacing material regression

### Scenario: A Cloudflare reference appears inside a page

- Given a page includes a Cloudflare mechanism or contextual-delivery concept
- When the page's implementation implications are assessed
- Then `grammar-family.html` supplies repository context
- And current official Cloudflare documentation supplies contemporary platform facts

### Scenario: A wide companion concentrates its content in one column

- Given a broad companion contains a narrative explanation and an independently useful set of onward links
- When the visitor has room to scan both groups together
- Then the reading considers adjacent groups under their shared heading
- And each group's internal reading order and prose measure remain legible
- And the narrow composition returns both groups to their authored sequence
- And a short introductory paragraph remains eligible for intentional open space

## Completion

Surface reading completes when the page can be explained through standing, visitor ability, evidence, ownership, fingerprint, and family relationships rather than title alone.
