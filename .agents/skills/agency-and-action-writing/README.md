# Agency and action writing

Use this skill to make prose say who acted, what they did, what changed, and why the change mattered. It restores clear agency without forcing first-person voice or replacing precise technical language.

## Start here

- Read the authoritative [skill contract](SKILL.md) before applying the method.
- Use it when passive construction, self-reference, entry metaphors, nominalization, or a generic container noun hides the actual work.
- Return the smallest faithful revision that preserves evidence strength, contribution boundaries, privacy, and the surrounding voice.

## Concrete example

**Situation:** A case study says, “The participant-authored account reports that the redesigned assignment workflow removed recurring errors.” The source establishes that the author performed the redesign and that the error pattern ended.

**Use:** Identify the actor, action, object, and consequence. Replace the self-reference with the supported action: “I redesigned the assignment workflow and eliminated the recurring assignment-error pattern.”

**Observable return:** A reader can recover the author’s contribution and the changed condition in one pass. The revision adds no claim beyond the source.

## Boundaries

Direct writing can use first person, team voice, a named system actor, or an accurate impersonal construction. Preserve quotations, governed terms, unknown or protected actors, and abstractions that identify a real boundary.
