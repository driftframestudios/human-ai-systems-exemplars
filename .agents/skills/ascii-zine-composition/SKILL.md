# ASCII zine composition

## Contract

- **What:** A restrained ASCII and typographic composition that helps a person read, act, and recover within an existing artifact.
- **When:** A studio, workshop, explanatory page, or editable artifact calls for zine character and has an identified visitor task and receiving style owner.
- **Boundaries:** The receiving application owns meaning, state, data, permissions, and interaction. The selected visual contract owns palette and tokens. This method selects and integrates ornament; it leaves scientific encodings and operational status with their source owners.
- **Result:** An expressive, source-owned composition with a complete semantic reading, recognizable controls, and focused evidence for the changed visitor behavior.

## Fit

The smallest useful dose is one masthead, a section-rule treatment, and one
annotation style. A dense measurement display can retain its existing technical
grammar while a neighboring introduction carries the publication character.
Monospace supports compact labels and texture; ordinary body text supports
sustained reading. A broad restyle earns its scope from the visitor task.

## Rules

### Rule: A mark has a declared job

Classify each accent as decoration, text, or a data encoding before rendering.
Decorative punctuation can be hidden from assistive technology and can shorten
or disappear at narrow widths. Meaningful ASCII artwork has an equivalent
ordinary-text reading or a suitably named image role. Values, units, scales,
sources, and uncertainty remain available when marks encode data. W3C's
[ASCII-art technique](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/html/H86) supplies
examples of text alternatives; a receiving artifact still needs its own check.

### Rule: Composition follows the reading

Mastheads establish purpose; rules separate actual sections; field notes group
related information; patch annotations explain a relationship or next step.
Short authored patterns accompany those structures. A stamp's words describe
its actual source or application state. A decorative badge alone supplies no
approval, achievement, capacity, confidence, or measured activity.
Plus signs indicate addition or deliberate diagram structure; ordinary headings
can stand plainly. Repeating an action-like mark as generic ornament can imply
an operation that the artifact does not provide.

### Rule: The receiving style owner keeps authority

Map the smallest needed palette, typography, spacing, and shadow roles into
the existing route or component owner. Preserve the selected visual contract's
light direction and semantic color roles. Distinguish portable reference tokens
from runtime selectors. A raw ASCII export uses its declared character set;
Unicode box drawing and block glyphs are a separately named extension with a
font and receiving-layout observation.

### Rule: Controls retain an ordinary operating grammar

Native buttons take actions, labeled field controls select values, and
disclosures reveal detail. Text names the affected object and consequence.
Removal and recovery can have a distinct color and border while keeping that
text. Keep accepted values, draft previews, and saved work visibly distinct.
Decorative framing leaves focus, target size, selection, and return routes
operable; controls remain outside hidden decorative containers.

### Rule: Texture yields to usable space

The authored reading reflows at the receiving width and text size. Decoration
crops or rests before meaningful text or controls lose space. A genuinely
two-dimensional diagram can keep a named local inspection region and semantic
companion. Print preserves the useful record. Forced colors retains labels,
states, and boundaries. Motion serves a named operation and offers a reduced
motion equivalent; stationary ornament is a complete default.

### Rule: Evidence follows the changed job

Inspect the undecorated reading and accessible names, then exercise the changed
task at its narrow and ordinary receiving widths. Add enlarged text, print,
forced colors, reduced motion, or another engine when that behavior changed or
a concrete observation calls for it. A screenshot establishes a rendered
composition; usability, conformance, and preference keep their fitting evidence
boundaries. Preserve useful failures as input to a bounded revision.

## Completion

The visitor can identify the artifact's purpose, read every material fact,
operate the changed controls, and recover from the affected action. The style
owner, source contract, ornament's job, and fitting observation are findable.
Further texture rests when it adds decoration without improving the chosen
composition. New visitor feedback, changed state behavior, or a receiving
layout difference can reopen the design.
