# Repository boundary checkpoint

## Desired receiving state

A repository owner can assess a declared collection of source, history,
collaboration records, and delivery material from one scoped receipt. Exact
findings stay with an authorized private receiver. Each unresolved category
has an owner and a next action; repository visibility stays private.

The assessment completes with one of three outcomes: **ready for declared
scope**, **remediation required**, or **coverage incomplete**. Completion of
an assessment and readiness of a repository are separate decisions. A
completed collection with privately assigned findings can establish the first
while keeping the second held.

[Checkpoint #489](https://github.com/driftframestudios/human-ai-systems-exemplars/issues/489)
owns the initial seven-repository exercise under milestone #20. A later
checkpoint supplies its own repository set, observation window, evidence
receiver, and grants. The [cross-repository cold-session docket](cross-repository-cold-session-docket.v0.1.md)
owns broader re-entry; this runbook orders the finite boundary assessment.

## Entry, authority, and safe continuation

Begin in `observation_only` mode. The coordinating collaborator confirms the
current branch, worktree state, nearest README, active Issue, remote visibility,
and authorized read access for every repository. Preserve existing work and
use an isolated acquisition area for history and hosted records.

Before acquisition, the checkpoint names:

- the human repository owner who receives the assessment;
- the source revisions, ref families, hosted surfaces, and collection window;
- an authorized private evidence receiver outside repositories, publication
  inputs, ordinary transcripts, and automatic synchronization;
- the permitted collectors, detector versions, finite resource bounds, and
  format readers;
- each support lane, its expected return, and stopping event; and
- the owner and retention or deletion event for private working evidence.

Before the first real record, prove the collector and private receiver with
synthetic success, command failure, parser failure, and unavailable-receiver
controls. Supporting and re-entry commands share this output boundary. Verify
that stdout, stderr, exceptions and support handbacks expose only the approved
finite categories while exact input and diagnostics stay private. A failed
control holds acquisition until the corrected path passes. Content-detector
canaries in Phase 2 still precede real-content interpretation.

A temporary local evidence area can support a bounded exercise when its
permissions and lifecycle are explicit. The coordinator arranges authorized
retention before the working area expires. Exact evidence stays private through
that transfer. A missing evidence receiver suspends acquisition; a missing
optional endpoint produces a scoped coverage entry and leaves independent
observations available.

Issue creation, source changes, push, and pull-request delivery use the current
task grant. Repository visibility, publication, deployment, merge, credential
administration, deletion of hosted revisions, and historical rewriting retain
their separate grants. A detected candidate pauses its affected handoff and
enters private triage. It does not authorize a destructive cleanup.

## Reference policy for this checkpoint family

Every Notion URL stays with the private evidence receiver, including a URL for
an otherwise eligible Registry page. Public-possible prose may name a page
whose membership in **Enterprise Human-AI Practitioner Registry** or its
descendants has been verified through native ancestry. A search hit, a
public-possible flag, an Atlas parent key, and a similarly named page each
supply useful discovery evidence; native ancestry supplies the membership
claim. Canonicalize native identifiers before comparing an ID returned as a
URL with one returned as a bare identifier. When representations differ,
resolve the native root ancestor and compare its canonical identity; equal
display titles alone leave membership unresolved. A catalog title match
supplies discovery evidence; when a reference carries a target identity,
verify that target even if another eligible page has the same title. A catalog miss remains
unresolved until a targeted observation resolves it.

An accepted public link uses the exact `kindlight.systems` route selected from
a reviewed Registry Atlas record or its accepted binding projection. Observe
the route signed out and check its final destination, response, title, and
meaning for the reader's task. Keep the Atlas version and selection evidence
separate from the live observation. A binding projection establishes its
selected records; the complete Atlas retains the wider corpus claim.

Other workspace references receive a local explanation, a fitting
repository-local source, or an operator-blind semantic key backed by the
private resolver. Preserve the useful decision and rationale. The private
mapping holds exact titles and coordinates where their disclosure standing
requires it.

## Phase 1 — Bind and enumerate

**Owner and mode:** The coordinator binds the checkpoint in `observation_only`
mode and assigns finite support packets. Each packet identifies its private
receiver and permits only category-level handbacks.

Use three independent collection lanes:

| Lane | Required inventory | Completeness evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Git source and history | Advertised branches, tags and PR refs; acquired reachable blobs, trees, commits and tags; historical filenames; LFS pointers and submodule references | Ref acquisition before and after, shallow/promisor standing, object integrity, object and byte counts, explicit external-object scope |
| Hosted collaboration | Issues and PRs in every state; bodies, conversations, reviews, inline comments and thread replies; available retained edits and attachments | Terminal pagination, stable IDs, deduplicated revisions, independent count reconciliation, attachment acquisition status |
| Delivery and metadata | Repository description and settings; labels and milestones; releases and assets; Actions runs, jobs, annotations, summaries, logs and artifacts; Pages and deployment records; enabled discussions, wiki and packages | Per-endpoint availability, terminal pagination, carrier inventory, retention state, format and resource limits |

The coordinator captures collection start and finish times. Git refs and hosted
record revisions have independent change clocks. End-of-window drift receives
a targeted re-read before the corresponding observation is settled.

The Git lane observes tracked generated carriers and distribution branches;
the delivery lane observes retained hosted copies. Named ignored or untracked
publication inputs need a separate local inventory or a reviewed rebuild.
Record unacquired working material explicitly: Git evidence establishes
versioned content, while those local inputs retain their own observation.
Historical names and commit or tag prose remain candidate carriers even when
current source has changed. Follow a submodule, LFS object, attachment or package
only through the granted receiver and acquired inventory; a pointer alone
leaves its target unobserved.

**Checkpoint:** Each inventory row has a repository, surface class, acquisition
state, count, private identity record, observation window and next receiver.
Record `observed`, `empty`, `disabled`, `expired`, `inaccessible`,
`unsupported`, `resource_limited`, or `changed_during_collection` with its
actual meaning. Empty requires a successful complete collection. An error or a
provider's absence response preserves the relevant coverage question.

**Recovery and stop:** Persist and verify each complete page or object batch
before advancing its resumable cursor. Bind the checkpoint to the repository,
endpoint or ref query, observation context, stable identities and revisions.
After interruption, reuse only a checkpoint whose preceding evidence is fully
retained and whose identity still matches. Uncertain persistence holds
completeness: reacquire from the last verified boundary and deduplicate by
stable identity and revision. A repeated cursor, inconsistent count, quota boundary,
malformed response or changing corpus holds that collection's completeness
claim. Continue independent lanes and return the exact bounded gap privately.

## Phase 2 — Prove detection and safe reporting

**Owner and mode:** Each lane proves its selected path before interpreting
real-corpus results. Repository-owned scanners retain their reviewed engine,
configuration, exceptions, and supported scopes.

Generate a fresh non-issued provider-shaped canary in transient input. Exercise
the material rule, finding exit, parser, redaction, and final safe-output path
together. Add synthetic path, workspace-URL, encoded-reference, title-policy,
and public-route controls for the distinct classifiers that the lane uses.
Positive controls establish those constructed cases; they leave real-world
recall and false-positive rates open.

Exercise malformed output, unreadable input, command failure, pagination
failure, truncation, unavailable format support and safe-report rejection.
Each operational failure preserves a coverage hold. Keep credential validity
checks disabled; the credential owner handles live-access questions.

Record normalization and resource limits before scanning: decoding depth,
maximum decoded bytes, archive member counts, expansion ratio, nesting depth,
timeouts and supported formats. Bind the host and root-identity constants to the current checkpoint and an
accepted Atlas record before exercising their controls. Parse hostnames and
URL structure rather than matching an incidental host substring. Inspect archives with contained readers
that reject traversal and symlink escapes; acquire bytes without executing
repository scripts, attachments, macros or embedded content as data.

**Checkpoint:** Controls prove the declared detector and reporting path, with
operational failures distinguishable from clean results. Raw diagnostics and
matched values remain private even when the report itself fails.

**Recovery and stop:** A failed control suspends interpretation for that path.
Repair or select a fitting collector within the current grant, repeat the
control, and then resume the real-corpus observation.

## Phase 3 — Inspect and classify

**Owner and mode:** Support lanes inspect their acquired corpora; the
coordinator joins reference and contextual evidence.

Perform distinct passes for credential-shaped material, machine or user
coordinates, Notion URLs, workspace-title standing, and embedded metadata.
Inspect structured fields, prose, filenames and decoded textual derivatives.
Format-aware readers cover declared image, document, media and archive classes;
a text search over binary bytes leaves compressed metadata and visible image
content outside its claim.

Contextual review distinguishes an actual workstation coordinate from an
intentional generic runtime example, a live-access candidate from an accepted
synthetic fixture, and authored content from provider-generated operational
metadata. Every candidate retains its private record and disposition rationale.
User policy decides whether a class is eligible; scanner silence and reviewed
credential exceptions leave the other classes' questions open. When opaque
raw-object files replace historical filenames for inspection, retain the
original paths privately: path-scoped exceptions can change behavior under
materialization, so contextual triage precedes a claim of a new credential
defect.

For title review, collect explicit workspace references and page-link labels,
compare against the verified ancestry catalog, and resolve ambiguous candidates
through targeted connector reads. Read context around policy-sensitive matches
and a declared sample of remaining prose. Name the sample and the limitations
of detecting implicit or differently worded references.

For embedded metadata, follow the
[format-aware publication guidance](../skills/secret-boundary-inspection/references/embedded-metadata-publication-boundary.md).
Preserve intentional attribution, color, orientation, accessibility and
provenance during any later source-owned repair. Audience fit and consent
remain human decisions with their own evidence.

**Checkpoint:** The private ledger joins a sequential opaque finding ID,
repository, surface, exact revision, category, evidence, disposition, owner and
next action. Keys use a public sequence, never a hash or encoding of sensitive
material. The outward projection contains only approved counts, categories,
coverage states and accountable receiver roles.

**Recovery and stop:** Ambiguous standing stays unresolved. A plausible active
credential routes privately to its owner before cleanup. A missing private
receiver holds the affected handoff while the coordinator restores the receiver.

## Phase 4 — Resolve the next action and renew evidence

**Owner and mode:** The coordinator returns actionable findings privately and
separates observation from each proposed repair grant.

Compare fitting options before converging: a source-owned replacement, local
explanation, paired private resolver, corrected generator, or bounded retained
historical treatment. A current-file edit changes that file's present state;
older commits, review revisions, logs, artifacts and copies retain their own
observation and response needs. A separately rooted, allowlisted projection
can establish readiness for that carrier. A branch or tag inside the same
repository leaves the repository's other accessible history in scope.

A focused authorized repair receives its own branch, regression evidence where
behavior changes, independent review, and private re-observation of changed
scopes. Historical rewriting, revision deletion, credential actions and
publication resume through the human owner's explicit grant. Keep the private
ledger's exact lineage intact across any approved repair.

**Checkpoint:** Every unresolved class has a named receiver, an actionable
next move, and its coverage or authorization requirement. Reacquire changed
refs, edited hosted bodies and regenerated deliveries independently.

**Recovery and stop:** A materially wider repair returns for a fresh scope
decision. The finite audit remains able to return remediation or coverage
friction while the wider readiness decision stays held.

## Phase 5 — Review, return, and renew

**Owner and mode:** An independent collaborator reviews collection
completeness, control results, dispositions, the safe-report boundary, and the
runbook's receiving exercise. Exact evidence is shared only through the
approved private receiver.

Use the checkpoint Issue or its focused PR as the single durable receipt:

```text
Observation window and repository set:
Visibility observed:
Declared surfaces and acquired counts:
Controls and normalized results:
Disposition: ready for declared scope | remediation required | coverage incomplete
Private remediation receiver:
Coverage limits and unresolved classes:
Independent review and receiving exercise:
Next event and accountable receiver:
```

Keep finding text, private titles, filenames, coordinates, raw errors,
screenshots, command traces and finding-derived fingerprints out of this
receipt. A category count is a routing observation, not a severity score or an
estimate of credential validity.

The reviewer can accept the assessment while keeping readiness held for
remediation or incomplete coverage. Close a checkpoint only when its stated
acceptance is satisfied or explicitly superseded by the accountable owner.
Keep the Issue open while its stated acceptance remains unsatisfied. Once
that finite checkpoint settles, retain the renewal triggers in this runbook
and bind the next triggered observation to a successor checkpoint Issue.

Renew the affected scope after source or ref changes, hosted edits, new
artifacts, changed Registry membership or public routes, detector/configuration
changes, private-receiver changes, or a proposed publication decision. Ordinary
recurrence is event-driven; a scheduled audit receives its own explicit grant.

can earn a shared collector or validator; the initial runbook composes existing
